Prefixes and vowel changes

A German prefix changes what a verb means, and whether it is separable decides where it goes in the sentence and how the participle looks.

Separable prefixes

Separable prefixes are stressed, jump to the end of a main clause (see Word order), and take -ge- in the middle of the participle: ankommen → Der Zug kommt an → Der Zug ist angekommen. Most of them are ordinary prepositions or adverbs, so their meaning is often guessable.

Form Example Translation
Present, main clause Ich stehe um sechs Uhr auf. I get up at six.
Perfekt Ich bin um sechs Uhr aufgestanden. I got up at six.
Subordinate clause ..., weil ich um sechs Uhr aufstehe. ... because I get up at six.
Modal verb Ich muss morgen früh aufstehen. I have to get up early tomorrow.
zu-infinitive Es ist schwer, früh aufzustehen. It's hard to get up early.

Inseparable prefixes

be-, ver-, er-, ent-, emp-, zer-, ge- and miss- never split off, are never stressed, and their participle has no ge-: besuchen → hat besucht, verstehen → hat verstanden. Their meanings are tendencies, not laws, but the tendencies help a lot.

Prefix Tendency Examples
be- makes a verb take a direct (Akk) object; "act on something" antworten auf → eine Frage beantworten; besuchen (visit), bezahlen (pay for), beschreiben (describe), bemerken (notice), bestellen (order), behalten (keep), bekommen (get, receive)
ver- (mistake) doing it wrong sich verlaufen (get lost on foot), sich verfahren (get lost driving), verschlafen (oversleep), sich versprechen (misspeak), sich verhören (mishear)
ver- (away, used up) losing, giving away, ending verlieren (lose), verkaufen (sell), vermieten (rent out), verbrauchen (use up), verschwinden (disappear), verlassen (leave)
ver- (change of state) making something more X verbessern (improve), vergrößern (enlarge), verlängern (extend), verändern (change)
ver- (no clear pattern) learn as vocabulary verstehen (understand), versuchen (try), verbringen (spend time), verdienen (earn), verbieten (forbid)
er- reaching a result erreichen (reach), erkennen (recognise), erklären (explain), erfinden (invent), erhalten (receive), erleben (experience), erzählen (tell), erwarten (expect)
ent- away, removing, undoing entfernen (remove), entdecken (discover, literally un-cover), entwickeln (develop, literally un-wrap), entlassen (dismiss), sich entspannen (relax), enttäuschen (disappoint), entscheiden (decide)
emp- ent- before f empfehlen (recommend), empfangen (receive, welcome), empfinden (feel)
zer- to pieces zerbrechen (shatter), zerstören (destroy), zerreißen (tear up), zerschneiden (cut up)
ge- no live meaning; fixed verbs gehören (belong, + Dat), gefallen (please, + Dat), gewinnen (win), genießen (enjoy), gelingen (succeed), gestehen (confess)
miss- wrongly, badly missverstehen (misunderstand), misstrauen (distrust, + Dat), misslingen (fail), missbrauchen (abuse)

  • zu-infinitive goes in front: Ich habe vergessen, dich anzurufen (separable) vs Ich versuche, es zu verstehen (inseparable).

Dual prefixes: über, unter, um, durch, wieder

These five can be separable or inseparable, and stress tells you which: stress on the prefix = separable, usually literal; stress on the verb = inseparable, usually figurative, with no ge- in the participle. Below, the stressed syllable is in CAPITALS.

Common verbs that come in only one version

Separable (prefix stressed) Inseparable (verb stressed)

Noun and adjective prefixes

Noun and adjective prefixes never change gender or word class; they only add meaning: un- = not (or very bad), ur- = original, Haupt- = main, Lieblings- = favourite. The gender still comes from the last part: der Film → der Lieblingsfilm.

Prefix Meaning Examples Note
un- (adjectives) not unglücklich, unmöglich, unbekannt, ungesund, unfreundlich, unhöflich, unklar, unabhängig Ukrainian не-: неможливий = unmöglich
un- (nouns) bad, negative; sometimes huge das Unglück (disaster; bad luck), der Unfall (accident), das Unwetter (severe storm), die Unruhe (unrest), die Unmenge (huge amount) Unwetter is not "no weather" but very bad weather
ur- original, very old, one generation further der Ursprung (origin), ursprünglich (originally), die Ursache (cause), der Urwald (primeval forest), uralt (ancient), die Urgroßmutter (great-grandmother), der Urenkel (great-grandson) Ukrainian пра-: прабабуся = Urgroßmutter; der Urlaub (holiday) is an old ur- word, now opaque
miss- failed, wrong das Missverständnis (misunderstanding), der Misserfolg (failure), das Misstrauen (distrust), der Missbrauch (abuse) pairs with miss- verbs
Haupt- main, chief der Hauptbahnhof (Hbf, main station), die Hauptstadt (capital), die Hauptsache (main thing), das Hauptgericht (main course), der Haupteingang (main entrance) hauptsächlich = mainly; Ukrainian головний, same image (Haupt = head)

  • un- with -bar and -lich is very productive: unglaublich (unbelievable), unvergesslich (unforgettable), unbezahlbar (unaffordable; priceless).

Vowel changes in word families

Two kinds of vowel change hide family links: Ablaut, the vowel series of strong verbs (sprechen – sprach – gesprochen; see Irregular verbs), which reappears in nouns made from them; and Umlaut (a → ä, o → ö, u → ü, au → äu), which appears in plurals, comparatives and many derived words. Once you see the pattern, Spruch and Gespräch are obviously sprechen.

Ukrainian vs German prefixes

A Ukrainian prefix usually makes a verb perfective, often without changing its meaning (писати → написати); a German prefix always changes the meaning and never marks aspect (schreiben → unterschreiben = to sign). German has no aspect: schreiben covers both писати and написати.

How German shows "finished" instead

  • Tense: Ich habe den Brief geschrieben = I wrote / have written the letter (написав).
  • Adverbs: schon (already), gerade (just now / right now), fertig: Ich bin fertig mit dem Buch.
  • A few result prefixes: aufessen, austrinken, durchlesen, aufräumen.
  • Ongoing action: gerade or am + infinitive (colloquial): Ich schreibe gerade einen Brief. / Ich bin am Schreiben. (я саме пишу)
  • Trap for Ukrainian speakers: do not look for a German prefix to make a verb "perfective". Adding one almost always gives a different verb: kaufen (buy) vs verkaufen (sell) vs einkaufen (shop).

Dual prefixes: the figurative pairs

At B2 the same rule as above runs through many more verbs: the stressed, separable version is literal; the unstressed, inseparable one is figurative. Stressed syllable in CAPITALS.

durchsetzen sich DURCHsetzen: to prevail, get one's way; hat sich durchgesetzt durchSETzen: to permeate; ein Text, von Fehlern durchsetzt
übergehen ÜBERgehen zu: to move on to; Wir gehen zum nächsten Punkt über. überGEhen: to pass over, ignore; Man hat mich bei der Beförderung übergangen.

verlegen / verwechseln misplace / mix up Ich habe meine Brille verlegt. Ich verwechsle die beiden immer.
  • ver- also means "to the end, to death" for slow processes: verhungern (starve), verdursten (die of thirst), verbluten (bleed to death), verblühen (wither).

Verb Meaning Example
(sich) erarbeiten earn or gain by work Sie hat sich ihren Erfolg hart erarbeitet.

  • er- with killing and dying marks the end result: erschießen (shoot dead), erstechen, erschlagen; ertrinken (drown), erfrieren (freeze to death), ersticken (suffocate). Compare schießen (shoot) and erschießen (shoot dead).

The stellen family at B2

The B1 table gave stellen's everyday prefixes; at B2 the same verbs gain abstract meanings, and a second set appears in formal writing.

Adjective prefixes

Adjective prefixes either negate (un-, miss-) or intensify (ur-, erz-, hoch-, tief-, stock-); the intensifiers differ mainly in register and in which adjectives they combine with.

Prefix Meaning Examples Register
un- not; often a polite understatement unschön (unpleasant), unweit (not far from), unzählig (countless); nicht unwichtig = quite important neutral
miss- badly, wrongly missverständlich (open to misunderstanding), missmutig (sullen), missgünstig (envious) neutral to formal
ur- very; deeply, originally urkomisch (hilarious), urplötzlich (all of a sudden), urgemütlich (very cosy), ureigen (very own) neutral, a little colloquial
erz- arch-, extremely (with -isms and beliefs) erzkonservativ, erzkatholisch, erzreaktionär neutral
hoch- highly (positive or technical) hochinteressant, hochbegabt (highly gifted), hochwertig (high quality), hochmodern, hochgiftig neutral, written
tief- deeply (feelings, colours, cold) tieftraurig, tiefgreifend (far-reaching), tiefblau, tiefgekühlt (frozen) neutral
stock- completely (mostly negative) stockdunkel (pitch-dark), stockkonservativ, stocktaub (stone-deaf), stocksauer (furious) colloquial
stink-, sau- extremely stinklangweilig, stinknormal, stinkreich; saukalt, saugut very colloquial
grund-, knall- totally; (colours) glaring grundverschieden (completely different), grundehrlich; knallrot, knallhart neutral / colloquial
  • un- works mainly on positive or neutral adjectives: unklug, unschön, but not *unschlecht.
  • Two negatives make a cautious positive, typical of formal German: Das ist nicht unproblematisch = this is rather problematic.
