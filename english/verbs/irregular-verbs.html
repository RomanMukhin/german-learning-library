Table of irregular verbs

Learn them in sound groups

Group verbs by pattern: i – a – u (drink, sing, swim, ring, sink); ought / aught (buy, bring, think, fight, catch, teach); no change (cut, put, hit, let, set, shut, cost). Learning a group at a time is faster than going alphabetically.
