Grammar is only half of the story. This area is about how English is used: how to ask for something politely, say a date, report what someone said, sound natural in conversation, choose between formal and informal styles and punctuate a piece of writing.

  • Could you possibly open the window? (polite request)
  • She told me (that) she was tired. (reported speech)
  • Well, you know, it's kind of complicated. (spoken English)

Notice the small words

Natural spoken English is full of short words and phrases like well, so, anyway, you know, I mean, sort of. They carry little meaning on their own, but they make you sound fluent and polite. Listen out for them.
