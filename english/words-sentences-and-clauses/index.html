This area looks at how words are built and how they combine into phrases, clauses and sentences. A clause has a subject and a verb (she left); a sentence has one or more clauses (She left because she was tired).

  • Unhappily, the meeting that we planned was cancelled. (prefix + suffix, relative clause, passive)
Unit Example
word happy
phrase very happy, the happy children
clause the children were happy
sentence The children were happy because it was snowing.

Find the verb first

To understand a long sentence, find each finite verb. Each one marks a clause. Then look for the words that join the clauses (and, because, which, if).
