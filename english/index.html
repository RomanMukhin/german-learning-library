This half of the library covers English grammar topic by topic, following the layout of a classic learner's grammar reference: eight large areas, each split into smaller topics. Every page explains the rule in plain words, gives short example sentences, and points out the traps.

  • Read the topics of an area in order, or jump to the one you need.
  • Each area ends with a quiz on its main page. Your last result is saved in this browser and shown next to the area's name.
  • Wrong forms are shown crossed out, so you can see at a glance what to avoid.

How to use the examples

Read every example sentence out loud. Grammar sticks much faster when you hear the pattern than when you only read the rule.
