Common mistakes in English

Word pattern mistakes

The structures that follow common words: afford to, apologise to, arrive at, avoid -ing, depend on, discuss, enjoy -ing, explain to, look forward to, suggest and many more.

Ability

  • the ability of doing → the ability to do

Able

  • I'm able of doing it. → I'm able to do it.

Affect someone or something

  • It affected on me. → It affected me. (no preposition) / It had an effect on me.

Afford

  • I can't afford buying it. → I can't afford to buy it.; I can't afford it.

Apologize to someone

  • I apologise you. → I apologise to you for the delay.

Approach

  • We approached to the house. → We approached the house. (verb, no to); an approach to the problem (noun + to)

Arrive somewhere

  • arrive to the airport → arrive at the airport, arrive in London, arrive home

Ask for

  • She asked a glass of water. → She asked for a glass of water.; She asked me for advice.

Attend

  • attend to a meeting → attend a meeting (no preposition); attend to = deal with

Avoid doing something

  • avoid to eat → avoid eating

Be/get dressed

  • I dressed me and left. → I got dressed and left. / She was dressed in black.

Because or because of?

  • because the rain → because of the rain / because it rained

Be worth doing something

  • It's worth to see. → It's worth seeing.

Build up or build?

  • They built up a new hospital. → They built a new hospital.; build up = increase gradually: build up confidence

Call

  • I'll call to you tomorrow. → I'll call you tomorrow. (phone); call at a place (Br, visit)

Can

  • I can to swim., I will can → I can swim., I'll be able to

Capable

  • capable to do → capable of doing

Come

  • I came to here. → I came here; Come and seeing → Come and see

Compensate

  • compensate the loss → compensate for the loss; compensate someone for something

Complain

  • complain for → complain about something, complain to someone

Concerning

  • concerning to your letter → concerning your letter (= about, formal)

Consider

  • consider about → consider the problem (no preposition)

Consider doing something

  • consider to move → consider moving

Contact

  • contact with me → contact me (verb, no preposition); be in contact with (noun)

Could

  • I could to go → I could go; for a single past success: I could pass the exam → I managed to / was able to pass

Depend on something

  • It depends of the weather. → It depends on the weather.; It's depend → It depends

Despite

  • despite of, despite it rained → despite the rain, despite the fact that it rained

Didn't

  • I didn't went. → I didn't go.

Die

  • He was died in 2001. → He died in 2001. / He is dead. (die of / from an illness are both correct)

Discuss

  • discuss about the problem → discuss the problem; have a discussion about

Dream

  • dream about to be → dream of / about being

Drop

  • a drop in 10% of sales → a drop of 10% in sales, sales dropped by 10%

Enjoy doing something

  • I enjoy to dance. → I enjoy dancing.

Enjoy yourself

  • I enjoyed very much. → I enjoyed myself / it very much.

Enter a place

  • enter in / into the room → enter the room; enter into an agreement (formal)

Explain something

  • explain me → explain it to me; explain that / how

Fall down or fall?

  • Prices fell down. → Prices fell.; fall down / over = fall to the ground

Find

  • I find it difficult to understanding → I find it difficult to understand

Give

  • give to me it → give it to me / give me it (informal)

Go

  • go to shopping → go shopping; go to home → go home

Have a relationship with someone

  • a relationship to my sister → a relationship with someone

Have difficulty doing something

  • have difficulty to find → have difficulty finding

Hear from sb

  • I look forward to hear you → I look forward to hearing from you.

Help

  • help me for → help me (to) do, help me with something

Hope

  • I hope you to come → I hope (that) you'll come / I hope to see you

Increase

  • increase of 10% in prices → an increase of 10% in prices; prices increased by 10%

Inform

  • inform about me, inform to me → inform someone of / about something, inform someone that

Interested

  • interested for learning, interested on → interested in learning

Invite someone to something

  • invite me for a party → invite someone to a party, invite someone to dinner

Join

  • join with us, join to the club → join us, join the club

Knock

  • knock the door → knock on / at the door

Lack

  • lack of as a verb → They lack experience. (verb, no of) / a lack of experience (noun)

Let

  • let me to go → let me go

Listen

  • listen music → listen to music

Look

  • look the picture → look at the picture; She looks happily → She looks happy

Look forward to

  • look forward to see → look forward to seeing

Make sb do sth

  • made me to wait → made me wait

May

  • It may rains. → It may rain.

Mean

  • What means this? → What does this mean?; I mean that is good (opinion) → I think it's good.

Mention

  • mention about → mention it, mention that

Might

  • might to come → might come

Mind

  • Do you mind to wait? → Do you mind waiting?

Must

  • must to go, musted → must go, had to

Need

  • You don't need come. → You don't need to come. / You needn't come.

Participate

  • participate to → participate in

Pay

  • pay the dinner → pay for the dinner; pay the bill / the rent (no for)

Phone

  • phone to my mother → phone my mother

Possibility

  • the possibility to win → the possibility of winning; the opportunity / chance to win

Prefer

  • prefer tea than coffee → prefer tea to coffee

Prevent

  • prevent to happen → prevent something (from) happening

Problem

  • I have a problem to understand → I have a problem understanding / I have problems with

Promise

  • promised me coming → promised to come / promised me that she would come

Provide

  • provide food to us (less usual), provide us of food → provide us with food / provide food for us

Reach

  • reach to the top → reach the top; reach for = stretch your hand

Recommend

  • recommend me to go → recommend going / recommend (that) I go / recommend a book to me

Regarding

  • regarding to → regarding your question (= about); with regard to

Relationship

  • relationship of → relationship with someone, relationship between two things

Rise up or rise?

  • Prices rose up. → Prices rose.

Search or search for?

  • search my keys → search for my keys (look for); search the house (look inside it)

Seek

  • seek for help → seek help (formal; no for)

Should

  • should to go → should go

Smile at someone/something

  • smile to me → smile at me

Spend

  • spend time to study → spend time studying; spend money on

Stay

  • stay at London → stay in a city / at a hotel / with friends

Stop doing something or stop to do something?

  • stop smoking = no longer smoke; stop to smoke = stop in order to smoke.

Succeed

  • succeed to do → succeed in doing

Suggest

  • suggest me to go → suggest (that) I go / suggest going

Suit

  • suit to you → suit you

Take care

  • take care about → take care of

Take part

  • take part on → take part in

Talk about or talk of?

  • talk about is the normal choice; talk of is formal or used in fixed phrases: talk of the devil.

Think

  • think to go → think of / about going

Think of doing something

  • I'm thinking to buy a car. → I'm thinking of buying a car.

Visit

  • visit to my aunt → visit my aunt; a visit to Paris (noun)

Wait

  • wait you → wait for you

Want

  • I want that you help → I want you to help

Want something/someone to do something

  • She wants me cooking. → She wants me to cook.

Way to do something

  • the way how to → the way to do it / the way of doing it / how to do it

Will

  • will to go, If it will rain → will go, If it rains

Would

  • If I would know → If I knew; I would to go → I would go

Would appreciate

  • I would appreciate if you could → I would appreciate it if you could…

Would like

  • I would like going → I would like to go
