Common verb ending mistakes

Mistake Correct Rule
stoped, planed stopped, planned double the consonant after one short stressed vowel
visitted, openned visited, opened don't double when the last syllable is unstressed (but preferred, stress on -fer)
makeing, writting making, writing drop final -e; write has a long vowel, so one t
studing, studyed studying, studied keep y before -ing; y → ied after a consonant
plaied played vowel + y: just add -ed
lieing, dieing lying, dying -ie → -ying

Hear the hidden letters

Say difficult words in an exaggerated way, the way they are spelt: Wed-NES-day, Feb-RU-ary, lib-RA-ry, gov-ERN-ment, en-vi-RON-ment. It helps you remember the silent or swallowed letters.
