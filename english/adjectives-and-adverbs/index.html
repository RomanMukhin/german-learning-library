Adjectives and adverbs

Words that describe things and actions: forms, order, position, comparison, and the small words that change their strength.

Adjectives describe people, things and ideas: a quiet street, The soup is hot. Adverbs add information about how, when, where or how much something happens, and they can also modify adjectives, other adverbs and whole sentences: She sings beautifully, Luckily, nobody was hurt.

Example a careful driver She drives carefully.
Typical form careful, happy, big carefully, happily, very, soon

Ask what the word describes

If it tells you about a thing or a person, you need an adjective. If it tells you about an action or another describing word, you need an adverb: He's a good cook but He cooks well.
