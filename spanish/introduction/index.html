This level is about getting things done in Spanish from day one. Each unit takes one everyday situation — saying hello, ordering a coffee, checking into a hotel, telling a doctor what hurts — and teaches the words you need for it together with the small piece of grammar that makes those words work: ser and estar, tú versus usted, me gusta, quiero, hace frío and more.

You will learn to… Key grammar along the way
greet people and say goodbye formal and informal you
say where you are from and what you do ser, nationality and profession endings
shop, order food and pay quiero, tengo, ¿cuánto cuesta?, numbers
talk about family, school, hobbies and chores possessives, me gusta, simple commands
travel, book a room, describe the weather hay, me gustaría, hace + weather

Learn phrases first, rules second

At this stage it pays to memorise whole chunks such as ¿Cómo te llamas? or ¿Me trae la cuenta? exactly as they are. The grammar notes in each unit explain why they look the way they do, so you can later build your own sentences.

Two kinds of Spanish, one language

Spanish is spoken in more than twenty countries. Where words differ (for example reserva in Spain and reservación in much of Latin America), the units point it out.
