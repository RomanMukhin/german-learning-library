This part of the library teaches Spanish grammar in five levels: everyday Introduction topics, then Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced grammar, and finally the main regional varieties of Spanish. Each level is split into units, and every unit explains its rules in plain English with Spanish examples and translations.

  • Work through a level's units in order, or jump to the one you need.
  • Each level ends with a quiz on its main page. Your last result is saved in this browser and shown next to the level's name.
  • Wrong forms are shown crossed out, so you can see at a glance what to avoid.

Say it out loud

Spanish spelling matches its pronunciation almost perfectly. Reading every example aloud trains your ear and your grammar at the same time.
