---
title: Collocation mistakes
sub: Words that go together: ask a question, make a mistake, do business, take an exam, give a speech and more.
order: 1
---
## Ask a question

» ~~make a question~~ / ~~do a question~~ → ***ask** a question*

We **ask** questions and **answer** them. *Can I **ask** you a question?*

## Big or great?

» ~~a big pleasure~~ → *a **great** pleasure*; ~~a big importance~~ → ***great** importance*

Use *great* with abstract nouns (*great importance, great interest, great fun*); *big* is mainly for physical size and *a big problem / mistake / decision*.

## Business

» ~~make business~~ → ***do** business*

*They **do** business with China.* Also: *run / start / set up a business*, *go out of business*.

## Change

» ~~do a change~~ → ***make** a change*

We *make* a change, *bring about* change, and something *undergoes* a change. *Change* the subject / your mind.

## Contribution

» ~~do a contribution~~ / ~~give a contribution to~~ → ***make** a contribution **to***

*She **made** an important **contribution to** the project.*

## Difficulty

» ~~I have difficulty to understand~~ → *I **have difficulty understanding*** / ***have difficulties with***

*Have difficulty / trouble* + *-ing*; *face / experience / overcome difficulties*.

## Do or make?

» ~~do a mistake~~, ~~make homework~~ → ***make** a mistake*, ***do** homework*

*Make* = create or produce; *do* = activities and work. See [Do or make?](easily-confused-words/b#do-or-make).

## Effort

» ~~do an effort~~ / ~~put an effort~~ → ***make** an effort*, ***put** effort **into***

*She **made** a real **effort** to be friendly.* *I **put** a lot of **effort into** this.*

## Experience

» ~~make an experience~~ → ***have** an experience*, ***gain / get** experience*

*I **had** a strange **experience** last night.* *You **gain** experience by working.*

## Friend

» ~~do friends~~ → ***make** friends (with)*

*It's easy to **make friends** at university.* *She **became friends with** her neighbour.*

## Improvement

» ~~do an improvement~~ → ***make** an improvement*, ***see / show** an improvement **in***

*There has been a big **improvement in** his work.*

## Make / give a speech

» ~~do a speech~~, ~~say a speech~~ → ***make / give** a speech*

Also *give a talk / a presentation / a lecture*.

## Mistake

» ~~do a mistake~~ → ***make** a mistake*

*Everyone **makes** mistakes.* *By mistake* = accidentally.

## Number

» ~~a big number of~~ → *a **large** number of*

*Number* goes with *large, small, high, low, growing*: *a **growing number** of people*.

## Party

» ~~make a party~~ → ***have / hold / throw** a party*

*We're **having** a party on Saturday.* *Throw a party* is informal.

## Profit

» ~~win a profit~~, ~~do a profit~~ → ***make** a profit*

*The company **made** a profit of £2 million.*

## Research

» ~~make research~~, ~~do a research~~ → ***do / carry out / conduct** research*

*Research* is uncountable: ***do research into / on** a subject*.

## Say or tell?

» ~~say a story / a lie / the truth~~ → ***tell** a story / a lie / the truth*

*Tell* collocates with *a story, a joke, a lie, the truth, the time, the difference*; *say* with *hello, goodbye, thank you, a prayer, something*.

## Speak or talk?

» ~~talk English~~ → ***speak** English*

*Speak* + language; *talk to / with* someone, *talk about* something, *give a talk*.

## Step

» ~~do a step~~ → ***take** a step*

*It's an important **step**.* ***Take** steps to solve the problem.*

## Success

» ~~make success~~, ~~have a success~~ → ***be** a success*, ***achieve** success*, ***have success** in*

*The party **was** a great success.*

## Suggestion

» ~~give a suggestion~~, ~~do a suggestion~~ → ***make** a suggestion*

*Can I **make** a suggestion?* *I have a suggestion.*

## Survey

» ~~make a survey~~ → ***carry out / conduct / do** a survey*

*We **carried out** a survey of 500 customers.*

## Take / sit an exam

» ~~make an exam~~, ~~do an exam~~ → ***take / sit** an exam* (Br)

You **take / sit** an exam; you **pass** or **fail** it. A teacher **gives / sets** an exam.

## Watch television

» ~~see television~~, ~~look television~~ → ***watch** television / TV*

But: *What's **on** TV?* *I saw it **on** TV.*

## Work

» ~~make a work~~, ~~do a job work~~ → ***do** work*, ***go to** work*, ***find / get** a job*

*Work* is usually uncountable: *I have a lot of **work** to do.*
