---
title: Determiner mistakes
sub: Missing and extra articles, much and many, hundred or hundreds, most or most of, this or that, and nouns like life, nature and society.
order: 3
---
## All + period of time

» ~~all the day~~ (less natural) → ***all day***, ***all week***, ***all night***

*I worked **all day**.* With *morning, afternoon, evening*: *all morning*.

## A long time

» ~~since long time~~, ~~for long time~~ → ***for a long time***

*I haven't seen her **for a long time**.* In questions and negatives *long* alone: *Have you waited **long**?*

## A lot of and lots of

» ~~a lot people~~, ~~lot of~~ → ***a lot of** people*, ***lots of** people*

Don't drop *of* before a noun or *a* before *lot*.

## Billions (of sth) or billion?

» ~~two billions people~~ → ***two billion** people*; ***billions of** people*

After a number, *billion* has no *-s* and no *of*.

## Countryside

» ~~a countryside~~, ~~in countryside~~ → ***the countryside***, *in **the** countryside*

*Countryside* is uncountable and normally takes *the*.

## Government

» ~~Government decided~~ → ***The government** decided*

Use *the* for a particular country's government.

## Hundreds or hundred?

» ~~three hundreds people~~ → ***three hundred** people*; ***hundreds of** people*

Same rule as *thousand, million, billion, dozen*.

## In future or in the future?

» British: ***in future*** = from now on: ***In future**, please be on time.* ***In the future*** = at some time later: ***In the future**, cars will drive themselves.*

American English uses *in the future* for both.

## Internet

» ~~on internet~~, ~~in the internet~~ → ***on the internet***

*I found it **on the internet**.* (Capital *I* is also common.)

## Life

» ~~The life is beautiful.~~ → ***Life** is beautiful.*

No article for life in general; use *the* for a specific life: *the life of a sailor*.

## Many, much, or a lot of?

» ~~I have much friends.~~ → *I have **a lot of** / **many** friends.* ~~I have much work.~~ (sounds unnatural) → *I have **a lot of** work.*

*Much* and *many* are most natural in questions and negatives.

## Millions or million?

» ~~five millions~~ → ***five million***; ***millions of** euros*

## Most of or most?

» ~~Most of people~~ → ***Most** people* / ***Most of the** people (in this room)*

*Most of* needs *the / my / these* or a pronoun.

## Nature

» ~~the nature~~ (= the natural world) → ***nature***

*I love being close to **nature**.* (no *the*) *The nature of* = the character of: *the nature of the problem*.

## Next or the next?

» ~~I'll see you the next week.~~ → *I'll see you **next week**.*

*Next week* counts from now. *The next week* = the week after a time in the past or the seven days from now.

## Other

» ~~others people~~ → ***other** people*; ~~an other~~ → ***another***

*Other* as an adjective never has *-s*.

## Same

» ~~same as~~, ~~a same~~ → ***the same** as*

*Same* almost always takes *the*: *We have **the same** phone.*

## Society

» ~~the society~~ (in general) → ***society***

*Crime is a problem for **society**.* With *the* only when specific: *the society of the 1920s*.

## Such

» ~~a such nice person~~, ~~such nice person~~ → ***such a** nice person*

*Such a / an* + singular noun; *such* + plural or uncountable: *such nice people*.

## Technology

» ~~the technology is changing our lives~~ (general) → ***Technology** is changing our lives.*

No article in general statements.

## This/that or these/those?

» ~~this people~~, ~~these kind~~ → ***these** people*, ***this** kind*

*This / that* + singular, *these / those* + plural.

## This/these or that/those?

*This / these* = near the speaker or the present moment; *that / those* = further away or in the past: *I like **this** song* (playing now) / *Do you remember **that** song?* (from before)

## Thousands or thousand?

» ~~two thousands~~ → ***two thousand***; ***thousands of** visitors*
