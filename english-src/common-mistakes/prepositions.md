---
title: Preposition mistakes
sub: The wrong preposition with times, places and nouns: on Monday afternoon, at home, on the beach, increase in, interest in, solution to.
order: 5
---
## Afternoon

» ~~at the afternoon~~, ~~on the afternoon~~ (general) → ***in** the afternoon*; ***on** Monday afternoon*

With a day: *on*. Otherwise: *in the morning / afternoon / evening*, *at night*.

## A lot

» ~~I like a lot of it~~ → *I like it **a lot***; ~~I learnt of a lot~~ → *I learnt **a lot***

No *of* when *a lot* is not followed by a noun.

## Attention

» ~~pay attention on~~ → *pay attention **to***

## Back to

» ~~go back home~~ (correct) vs ~~go back to home~~ → *go back **home***; *go back **to** school / work*

No *to* before *home*.

## Beach

» ~~in the beach~~ → ***on** the beach*; *go **to** the beach*; *at the beach* (= at the seaside)

## Course

» ~~do a course of English~~ → *do a course **in** English*; *a course **on** history*; ***on** a course*

## Day

» ~~in Monday~~, ~~at my birthday~~ → ***on** Monday*, ***on** my birthday*, ***on** that day*

## Decrease

» ~~a decrease of sales~~ → *a decrease **in** sales*; *decrease **by** 10%* / *decrease **to** 200*

## Difference

» ~~the difference of A and B~~ → *the difference **between** A and B*; *make a difference **to***

## Experience

» ~~experience on teaching~~ → *experience **in / of** + -ing or noun*; *experience **with** computers*

*I have experience **in** teaching.* *She has **experience of** working abroad.*

## Floor

» ~~in the second floor~~ → ***on** the second floor*; ***on** the floor* (surface)

## Home

» ~~I'm going to home.~~, ~~I'm in home.~~ → *I'm going **home**.* / *I'm **at home**.*

## Increase in or increase of?

» ~~an increase of prices~~ → *an increase **in** prices*; *an increase **of** 5%* (the amount)

## Interest

» ~~interest for~~, ~~interested on~~ → *interest **in***, *interested **in***

## Island

» ~~in the island~~ (usual) → ***on** the island*; ***on** Crete*

## Knowledge

» ~~knowledge on~~ → *knowledge **of***; *to my knowledge*

*She has a good **knowledge of** French.*

## Last but not least

» ~~at last but not least~~ → ***Last but not least***, …

Fixed phrase, no preposition.

## Leave

» ~~leave from the room~~ (not usual) → ***leave** the room*; *leave **for** London* (destination)

## Need

» ~~need of help~~ (formal only) → *need help*; *be **in need of***; *a need **for***

## Newspaper

» ~~I read it on the newspaper.~~ → *I read it **in** the newspaper.*

But ***on** TV, **on** the radio, **on** the internet*.

## Occasion

» ~~in this occasion~~ → ***on** this occasion*, ***on** special occasions*

## On the one hand ... on the other hand

» ~~in one hand~~, ~~from the other hand~~ → ***On the one hand** … **on the other hand** …*

## Opinion

» ~~according to my opinion~~, ~~on my opinion~~ → ***in** my opinion*

## Pleased

» ~~pleased of~~ → *pleased **with** (something / someone)*, *pleased **to** meet you*, *pleased **about***

## Point of view

» ~~in my point of view~~ → ***from** my point of view*

## Reason

» ~~the reason of~~ → *the reason **for***

## Responsible

» ~~responsible of~~ → *responsible **for***

## Rise

» ~~a rise of prices~~ → *a rise **in** prices*; *rise **by** 3%*

## Row

» ~~on the first row~~ → ***in** the first / front row*; ~~three days in row~~ → *three days **in a row*** (= one after another)

## Solution

» ~~solution for the problem~~ (common), ~~solution of~~ → *solution **to** the problem*

## Take into consideration

» ~~take in consideration~~ → *take **into** consideration*, *take **into** account*

## Television

» ~~in TV~~ → ***on** TV / television*; *watch TV*

## Train

» ~~I came with the train.~~ → *I came **by** train.* / *I was **on** the train.*

## World

» ~~the biggest of the world~~ → *the biggest **in** the world*; *all **over** the world*; *around the world*

## Worry

» ~~worry for~~ → *worry **about***; *worried **about***
