---
title: Part of speech mistakes
sub: Using a noun instead of a verb, an adjective instead of an adverb and similar mix-ups: advice/advise, breath/breathe, good/well, life/live, your/yours.
order: 4
---
## Advice or advise?

» ~~I advice you to rest.~~ → *I **advise** you to rest.* (verb) / *Thanks for the **advice**.* (noun)

## Affect or effect?

» ~~It will effect everyone.~~ → *It will **affect** everyone.* (verb) / *It will have an **effect** on everyone.* (noun)

## Apologize or apology?

» ~~I apology for the delay.~~ → *I **apologise / apologize** for the delay.* (verb) / *Please accept my **apology**.* (noun)

## Breath or breathe?

» ~~Breath deeply.~~ → ***Breathe** /briːð/ deeply.* (verb) / *Take a deep **breath** /breθ/.* (noun)

## Choice or choose?

» ~~You have to choice.~~ → *You have to **choose**.* (verb; past *chose*, participle *chosen*) / *It's your **choice**.* (noun)

## Complaint or complain?

» ~~I want to complaint.~~ → *I want to **complain**.* (verb) / *I want to make a **complaint**.* (noun)

## Completely

» ~~It's complete different.~~ → *It's **completely** different.*

Use the adverb before an adjective.

## Concern or concerned?

» ~~I am concern about it.~~ → *I am **concerned** about it.* (adjective) / *It's a big **concern**.* (noun)

## Died or dead?

» ~~My grandfather is died.~~ → *My grandfather **is dead*** / ***died** in 2010* / ***has died***.

*Dead* is the adjective; *died* is the past of *die*.

## Different or difference?

» ~~What's the different?~~ → *What's the **difference**?* (noun) / *They are **different**.* (adjective)

## Easy or easily?

» ~~I passed easy.~~ → *I passed **easily**.* (adverb) / *The test was **easy**.* (adjective)

## Good or well?

» ~~She plays good.~~ → *She plays **well**.* (adverb) / *She's a **good** player.* (adjective)

*I'm well* = healthy; *I feel good* = happy / positive.

## Healthy or health?

» ~~Smoking is bad for your healthy.~~ → *…for your **health**.* (noun) / *a **healthy** diet* (adjective)

## Important

» ~~It's very importance.~~ → *It's very **important**.* (adjective) / *the **importance** of sleep* (noun)

## Inconvenient or inconvenience?

» ~~Sorry for the inconvenient.~~ → *Sorry for the **inconvenience**.* (noun) / *an **inconvenient** time* (adjective)

## Life or live?

» ~~I want to life in London.~~ → *I want to **live** /lɪv/ in London.* (verb) / *my **life*** (noun; plural *lives*) / ***live** /laɪv/ music* (adjective)

## Lost or loss?

» ~~It was a big lost.~~ → *It was a big **loss**.* (noun) / *I **lost** my keys.* (verb)

## Open and close

» ~~The shop is opened.~~ (state) → *The shop is **open**.* / *The shop is **closed**.*

*Open* is the adjective; *close* /kləʊs/ as an adjective means *near*.

## Others, other, or another?

» ~~others people~~ → ***other** people*; ~~an other day~~ → ***another** day*; *Some came, **others** didn't.* (pronoun)

## Please or pleased?

» ~~I'm please to meet you.~~ → *I'm **pleased** to meet you.* (adjective) / ***Please** sit down.*

## Relax or relaxed?

» ~~I feel relax.~~ → *I feel **relaxed**.* (adjective) / *I want to **relax**.* (verb)

## Remember or memory?

» ~~I have good remembers of it.~~ → *I have good **memories** of it.* (noun) / *I **remember** it well.* (verb)

## Safe or save?

» ~~Keep it save.~~ → *Keep it **safe**.* (adjective) / ***Save** your work.* / *save money* (verb)

## Sale or sell?

» ~~They sale cars.~~ → *They **sell** cars.* (verb) / *The house is **for sale**.* (noun)

## Slightly or slight?

» ~~It's slight better.~~ → *It's **slightly** better.* (adverb) / *a **slight** problem* (adjective)

## Truth or true?

» ~~Is it truth?~~ → *Is it **true**?* (adjective) / *Tell me the **truth**.* (noun)

## You or your?

» ~~What's you name?~~ → *What's **your** name?* (possessive determiner) / ***You** are late.* (pronoun)

## Your or yours?

» ~~Is this book your?~~ → *Is this book **yours**?* (pronoun) / *Is this **your** book?* (before a noun)
