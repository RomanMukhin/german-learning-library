---
title: Word order mistakes
sub: Where to put adverbs like again, already, also, always, just, often, only, still and very much, and the order in indirect questions with how, what, where and why.
order: 8
---
## Again

» ~~I again saw her.~~ → *I saw her **again**.*

*Again* goes at the end of the clause.

## All

» ~~All we are tired.~~ → *We are **all** tired.* / ***All of us** are tired.*

*All* goes after *be* and auxiliaries, before main verbs: *We **all** like it.*

## Almost

» ~~I almost have finished.~~ → *I have **almost** finished.*; ~~Almost all of people~~ → ***Almost all** people*

## A lot

» ~~I a lot like it.~~, ~~I like a lot it.~~ → *I like it **a lot**.*

## Already

» ~~I already have done it.~~ → *I have **already** done it.*

Mid position: after the first auxiliary.

## Also

» ~~I like also jazz.~~ → *I **also** like jazz.*; ~~Also I am a student~~ (informal) → *I am **also** a student.*

Before the main verb, after *be* and auxiliaries.

## Always

» ~~I go always to bed late.~~ → *I **always** go to bed late.*; ~~She always is happy~~ → *She is **always** happy.*

## As well

» ~~I as well like it.~~ → *I like it **as well**.*

*As well* goes at the end.

## Better

» ~~I better like tea.~~ → *I like tea **better*** / *I **prefer** tea.*; ~~You better go~~ → *You'**d better** go.*

## Both

» ~~Both they are doctors.~~ → *They are **both** doctors.* / ***Both of them** are doctors.*

## Enough

» ~~It's not enough big.~~ → *It's not **big enough**.*; *We don't have **enough money*** (before nouns).

## Even

» ~~Even he didn't call me.~~ (= surprisingly, not even a call) → *He didn't **even** call me.*

Put *even* next to what is surprising.

## Hardly

» ~~I hardly can believe it.~~ → *I can **hardly** believe it.*

After the first auxiliary or modal.

## How

» ~~Do you know how old is she?~~ → *Do you know **how old she is**?*; ~~How you are?~~ → ***How are you**?*

## Just

» ~~I have finished just.~~ → *I have **just** finished.*

## Never

» ~~I have seen never snow.~~ → *I have **never** seen snow.*; ~~Never I eat meat~~ → *I **never** eat meat.*

## Often

» ~~I go often to the cinema.~~ → *I **often** go to the cinema.* (or *I go to the cinema **often***)

## Only

» ~~Only I have one brother.~~ (= nobody else has one) → *I **only** have one brother.* / *I have **only** one brother.*

Put *only* before the word it focuses on.

## Still

» ~~I live still with my parents.~~ → *I **still** live with my parents.*; ~~I haven't still finished~~ → *I **still** haven't finished.*

## Very much

» ~~I very much like it.~~ (formal), ~~I like very much it.~~ → *I like it **very much**.*

After the object.

## What

» ~~I don't know what does it mean.~~ → *I don't know **what it means**.*; ~~What means this word?~~ → ***What does** this word **mean**?*

## Where

» ~~Can you tell me where is the bank?~~ → *Can you tell me **where the bank is**?*

## Why

» ~~Why you didn't come?~~ → ***Why didn't you** come?*; ~~I asked why was she late~~ → *I asked **why she was** late.*
