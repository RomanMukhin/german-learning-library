Many English words are confused because they look similar (*affect / effect*), sound the same (*their / there / they're*), or translate to one word in your language (*borrow / lend*, *say / tell*). Each entry below shows the difference in a few lines with examples.

> [!hack] Learn the pair, not the word
> When you look up one of these words, learn its partner at the same time, with one example sentence for each. Contrasting them is what makes the difference stick.

The entries are in alphabetical order, split over five pages.
