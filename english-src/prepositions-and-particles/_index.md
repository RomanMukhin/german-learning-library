**Prepositions** are short words like *in, on, at, by, for, with, from* that link a noun phrase to the rest of the sentence. They show relationships of place, time, direction, cause, means and many abstract ideas. The same words can also be **particles** in multi-word verbs: *look **up**, give **in**, get **on** with*.

» The keys are **on** the table. (place)
» I'll see you **at** six. (time)
» She came **by** train. (means)

> [!trap] Don't translate prepositions
> Prepositions rarely match one-to-one between languages. Learn them in chunks: *good **at***, *interested **in***, *depend **on***, *on Monday*, *at night*, *in the morning*.
