**Nouns** name people, things, places and ideas (*teacher, chair, London, freedom*). **Pronouns** stand in for nouns (*she, it, someone, which*). **Determiners** come at the start of a noun phrase and tell us which one or how much (*the, my, this, some, every, many*).

» **The** **old** **house** **on the corner** is for sale. (determiner + adjective + noun + postmodifier)
» **She** bought **it** last year. (pronouns)

> [!hack] Countable or uncountable first
> Before choosing an article or a quantifier, ask whether the noun is countable (*a chair, two chairs*) or uncountable (*furniture*). That one decision controls *a/an*, plurals, *much/many* and *few/little*.
