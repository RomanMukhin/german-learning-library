## How

### In questions

*How* asks about manner, condition, and (with adjectives and adverbs) degree:

» **How** do you spell it? / **How** are you? / **How** was the trip?
» **How old**, **how long**, **how far**, **how often**, **how much / many**

### In indirect questions and exclamations

» *I don't know **how** it works.* (normal word order: subject + verb)
» ***How** beautiful!* ***How** kind of you!* (exclamation)

### How about …?

*How about* + noun or *-ing* makes a suggestion: ***How about** going to the cinema?*

### Typical errors

» ~~How is called this?~~ → ***What** is this called?*
» ~~How does it look like?~~ → ***What** does it look like?* / ***How** does it look?*

## What

### As a question word

*What* asks about things and information: ***What** happened?* ***What** do you want?* ***What** time is it?* (as a determiner) ***What** for?* / ***What** … **for**?* asks about purpose: ***What** is this button **for**?*

### What meaning "please repeat"

*What?* is informal and can sound rude; ***Sorry?*** or ***Pardon?*** are more polite.

### Emphatic questions

***What on earth** are you doing?* ***Whatever** made you say that?* (surprise or annoyance)

### As a pronoun

*What* can mean "the thing(s) that": ***What** I need is a holiday.* *I'll do **what** I can.*

### Typical error

» ~~The thing what I want~~ → *The thing **that** I want* / ***What** I want*

## When

### As a question word

***When** is your birthday?* ***When** did you arrive?* ***Since when**…?* often shows surprise or annoyance.

### As a conjunction

It introduces time clauses. For the **future**, use a present tense after *when*:

» I'll call you **when** I **arrive**. Not: ~~when I will arrive~~
» **When** I was a child, I lived in York. (past)
» **When** it rains, the road floods. (general present)

### As a relative pronoun

*I remember the day **when** we met.*

### When or if? When or since?

*When* for things that will certainly happen, *if* for possibilities. *Since* (not *when*) is used with the present perfect for a starting point: *I've lived here **since** I was ten.*

## Where

### As a question word

***Where** do you live?* ***Where** are you from?* ***Where** are you going (to)?*

### Emphatic questions

***Where on earth** have you been?* ***Wherever** did you find it?*

### As a conjunction and relative pronoun

» *Put it **where** I can see it.* (place)
» *This is the house **where** I was born.* (= in which)

## Which

### As a question word

*Which* asks people to choose from a limited set and can be a determiner or a pronoun: ***Which** colour do you prefer?* ***Which** is yours?*

### Which or what?

*Which* for a known, limited choice; *what* for an open choice: ***What** films do you like?* / ***Which** film shall we watch, this or that?*

### In relative clauses

*the car **which** I bought*; *He failed, **which** was a shock.* (refers to the whole clause)

### Which of

***Which of** you* / ***which of** these books*; *which* + *of* is needed before a determiner or pronoun.

## Who, whom

### Who as a question word

*Who* can be subject or object: ***Who** called?* (subject: no *did*) / ***Who** did you call?* (object). ***Who on earth** told you that?*

### Who in relative clauses

*The woman **who** lives next door is a doctor.*

### Whom

*Whom* is the formal object form, mainly after prepositions or in formal writing: *To **whom** it may concern.* *The man **with whom** she works.* In speech we usually use *who* or leave the pronoun out.

## Whose

### As a question word

*Whose* asks about the owner: ***Whose** is this?* ***Whose** bag is this?*

### In relative clauses

*the girl **whose** brother is famous*; *a company **whose** profits are falling* (also for things).

### Typical error

» Don't confuse with *who's* (= who is / who has): ***Who's** coming?* / ***Whose** coat is this?*

## Why

### Asking for reasons and giving them

***Why** are you late?* — ***Because** the bus was late.* ***That's why** …* gives a reason: *I missed the bus. **That's why** I'm late.*

### Why should …? Why don't …? Why not?

» ***Why should** I help him?* (annoyance)
» ***Why don't** we go out?* / ***Why not** try again?* (suggestions; *why not* + bare infinitive)
» *"Shall we eat out?" "**Why not**?"* (agreement)

### Typical error

» ~~Why you are late?~~ → ***Why are you** late?* (question word order)
