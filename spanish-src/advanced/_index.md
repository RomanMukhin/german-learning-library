This level takes you from solid intermediate Spanish to confident, nuanced Spanish. It is built around three big ideas: **compound tenses** made with *haber* and *estar*, ways of talking about actions **without naming the doer**, and the **subjunctive mood**.

| Building block | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| perfect tenses | *haber* + past participle | *He visto*, *había visto*, *habré visto*, *habría visto* |
| progressive tenses | *estar* + present participle | *Estaba leyendo*, *estuve leyendo*, *estaré leyendo* |
| subjunctive perfects | *haya / hubiera* + past participle | *que haya visto*, *si hubiera visto* |

> [!hack] Learn the participles once
> Every perfect tense uses the same past participles, and every progressive tense uses the same *gerundio*. Master [Present Participles](advanced/present-participles) and [Past Participles](advanced/past-participles) early and the later units become a matter of swapping the helper verb.

> [!fact] WEIRDO
> The subjunctive units return again and again to the same triggers: **W**ishes, **E**motions, **I**mpersonal expressions, **R**ecommendations, **D**oubt and denial, and **O**jalá.
