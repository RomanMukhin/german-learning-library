## Regular Verbs

The Spanish present tense (*el presente*) covers English "I speak", "I am speaking", "I do speak" and often "I'm going to speak". It's used for habits, facts, things happening now, near-future plans, and ordering in shops and restaurants.

To form it, take the infinitive, remove *-ar*, *-er* or *-ir* to find the **stem**, and add the ending for the subject.

| Subject | *hablar* — speak | *comer* — eat | *vivir* — live |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *hablo* | *como* | *vivo* |
| *tú* | *hablas* | *comes* | *vives* |
| *él, ella, usted* | *habla* | *come* | *vive* |
| *nosotros/as* | *hablamos* | *comemos* | *vivimos* |
| *vosotros/as* | *habláis* | *coméis* | *vivís* |
| *ellos, ellas, ustedes* | *hablan* | *comen* | *viven* |

*-er* and *-ir* verbs share all their endings except *nosotros* and *vosotros*.

» *Trabajo en una oficina.* — I work in an office. (habit)
» *El agua hierve a cien grados.* — Water boils at 100 degrees. (fact)
» *¿Qué lees?* — What are you reading? (now)
» *Mañana salimos para Lima.* — Tomorrow we leave for Lima. (near future)
» *Para mí, una sopa.* / *Quiero una sopa.* — I'll have a soup. (ordering)

### Present + desde hace: "have been doing"

To say how long something has been going on, Spanish uses the present, where English uses the present perfect:

» *Vivo en Madrid desde hace tres años.* — I've been living in Madrid for three years.
» *Hace dos horas que espero.* — I've been waiting for two hours.

> [!hack] The vowel tells the family
> *-ar* verbs use *a* in their endings (*-as, -a, -an*); *-er* and *-ir* verbs use *e* (*-es, -e, -en*). If you mix them up you'll often land in another tense, so this one matters.

## Irregular Verbs

Some of the most frequent verbs are irregular. Learn these as whole tables.

| | *ser* — be | *estar* — be | *ir* — go | *tener* — have | *haber* (there is) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *soy* | *estoy* | *voy* | *tengo* | — |
| *tú* | *eres* | *estás* | *vas* | *tienes* | — |
| *él, ella, Ud.* | *es* | *está* | *va* | *tiene* | *hay* |
| *nosotros/as* | *somos* | *estamos* | *vamos* | *tenemos* | — |
| *vosotros/as* | *sois* | *estáis* | *vais* | *tenéis* | — |
| *ellos, ellas, Uds.* | *son* | *están* | *van* | *tienen* | — |

### Irregular yo forms

Many verbs are regular **except** in the *yo* form:

| Pattern | Verbs | *yo* form |
|---|---|---|
| *-go* | *hacer, poner, salir, traer, valer* | *hago, pongo, salgo, traigo, valgo* |
| *-go* + stem change | *tener, venir, decir* | *tengo, vengo, digo* (then *tienes, vienes, dices*) |
| *-oy* | *dar, estar, ir, ser* | *doy, estoy, voy, soy* |
| *-zco* | *conocer, conducir, traducir, parecer* | *conozco, conduzco, traduzco, parezco* |
| others | *saber, ver, caber* | *sé, veo, quepo* |

» *Hago la cena y pongo la mesa.* — I make dinner and set the table.
» *No sé dónde está.* — I don't know where it is.
» *Conozco a tu hermana.* — I know your sister.

### Spelling changes in yo

Some verbs change a letter to keep the same sound: *escoger → escojo*, *seguir → sigo*, *distinguir → distingo*.

> [!trap] Hay
> *hay* means "there is / there are" and never changes for singular or plural: *Hay un perro.* / *Hay tres perros.* ~~*Hayn tres perros.*~~

## Stem-Changing Verbs 1

Many verbs change the vowel of their stem when it is stressed. The endings stay regular. The change happens in every form **except nosotros and vosotros** — draw the shape on the table and you get a "boot".

### e → ie

| | *querer* — want | *pensar* — think | *preferir* — prefer |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *quiero* | *pienso* | *prefiero* |
| *tú* | *quieres* | *piensas* | *prefieres* |
| *él, ella, Ud.* | *quiere* | *piensa* | *prefiere* |
| *nosotros/as* | *queremos* | *pensamos* | *preferimos* |
| *vosotros/as* | *queréis* | *pensáis* | *preferís* |
| *ellos, ellas, Uds.* | *quieren* | *piensan* | *prefieren* |

Others like this: *empezar* (begin), *cerrar* (close), *entender* (understand), *perder* (lose), *sentir* (feel), *mentir* (lie). *tener* and *venir* combine this change with their irregular *yo* forms.

» *¿A qué hora empieza la película?* — What time does the film start?
» *No entiendo la pregunta.* — I don't understand the question.
» *Si no salimos ya, perdemos el tren.* — If we don't leave now, we'll miss the train.

> [!hack] Stress drives the change
> The vowel only changes in syllables that get the stress: *QUIE-ro*, but *que-RE-mos*. Saying the forms aloud makes the "boot" pattern obvious.

## Stem-Changing Verbs 2

The other two stem-change groups follow the same boot pattern.

### o → ue

| | *poder* — can | *dormir* — sleep | *volver* — return |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *puedo* | *duermo* | *vuelvo* |
| *tú* | *puedes* | *duermes* | *vuelves* |
| *él, ella, Ud.* | *puede* | *duerme* | *vuelve* |
| *nosotros/as* | *podemos* | *dormimos* | *volvemos* |
| *vosotros/as* | *podéis* | *dormís* | *volvéis* |
| *ellos, ellas, Uds.* | *pueden* | *duermen* | *vuelven* |

Also: *contar* (count, tell), *encontrar* (find), *recordar* (remember), *costar* (cost), *almorzar* (have lunch), *morir* (die). *jugar* is the only *u → ue* verb: *juego, juegas, juega, jugamos, jugáis, juegan*.

### e → i (only -ir verbs)

| | *pedir* — ask for | *repetir* — repeat | *servir* — serve |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *pido* | *repito* | *sirvo* |
| *tú* | *pides* | *repites* | *sirves* |
| *él, ella, Ud.* | *pide* | *repite* | *sirve* |
| *nosotros/as* | *pedimos* | *repetimos* | *servimos* |
| *vosotros/as* | *pedís* | *repetís* | *servís* |
| *ellos, ellas, Uds.* | *piden* | *repiten* | *sirven* |

Also: *seguir* (*sigo*), *vestir* (dress), *elegir* (*elijo*), *decir* (*digo, dices*).

» *¿Cuánto cuesta este abrigo?* — How much does this coat cost?
» *Los niños duermen la siesta.* — The children are having a nap.
» *Siempre pido lo mismo.* — I always order the same thing.

> [!trap] No stem change for nosotros
> ~~*nosotros puedemos*~~ → *podemos*; ~~*nosotros duermimos*~~ → *dormimos*.

## Comprehensive Review 1

Focus: regular endings.

| | *-ar* | *-er* | *-ir* |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *-o* | *-o* | *-o* |
| *tú* | *-as* | *-es* | *-es* |
| *él/ella/Ud.* | *-a* | *-e* | *-e* |
| *nosotros* | *-amos* | *-emos* | *-imos* |
| *vosotros* | *-áis* | *-éis* | *-ís* |
| *ellos/Uds.* | *-an* | *-en* | *-en* |

### Practice

» *Mis padres (cocinar) ___ muy bien.* → *cocinan* — My parents cook very well.
» *¿Tú (beber) ___ café?* → *bebes* — Do you drink coffee?
» *Nosotros (escribir) ___ correos.* → *escribimos* — We write emails.
» *Yo (estudiar) ___ por la noche.* → *estudio* — I study at night.

## Comprehensive Review 2

Focus: irregular *yo* forms.

### Practice

» *Yo (salir) ___ a las ocho.* → *salgo* — I leave at eight.
» *Yo no (saber) ___ nadar.* → *sé* — I can't swim.
» *Yo (conducir) ___ un coche pequeño.* → *conduzco* — I drive a small car.
» *Yo (traer) ___ el postre.* → *traigo* — I'll bring dessert.
» *Yo (ver) ___ la tele.* → *veo* — I watch TV.
» *Yo (dar) ___ clases de piano.* → *doy* — I give piano lessons.

## Comprehensive Review 3

Focus: stem changes.

### Practice

» *Ella (cerrar) ___ la ventana.* → *cierra* — She closes the window.
» *¿(Poder, tú) ___ ayudarme?* → *Puedes* — Can you help me?
» *Nosotros (jugar) ___ al fútbol.* → *jugamos* — We play football (no change).
» *El camarero (servir) ___ la comida.* → *sirve* — The waiter serves the food.
» *Yo no (recordar) ___ su nombre.* → *recuerdo* — I don't remember his name.
» *¿Qué (preferir) ___ ustedes?* → *prefieren* — What do you prefer?

## Comprehensive Review 4

Focus: uses of the present.

» *Siempre desayuno a las siete.* — I always have breakfast at seven. (habit)
» *Los gatos ven en la oscuridad.* — Cats can see in the dark. (general truth)
» *El sábado vamos a la playa.* — On Saturday we're going to the beach. (plan)
» *Si llueve, nos quedamos en casa.* — If it rains, we'll stay home. (condition with *si*)
» *Estudio inglés desde hace un año.* — I've been studying English for a year. (duration)
» *¿Te ayudo?* — Shall I help you? (offer)

> [!hack] Offers with the present
> Spanish asks "Shall I...?" with a plain present question: *¿Abro la ventana?* — Shall I open the window?

## Extra Practice 1

Mixed regular and irregular verbs. Read each sentence aloud and check the form.

» *Mi hermano (tener) ___ veinte años.* → *tiene* — My brother is twenty.
» *Nosotros (ir) ___ al mercado los sábados.* → *vamos* — We go to the market on Saturdays.
» *¿De dónde (ser) ___ tú?* → *eres* — Where are you from?
» *Ellos (vivir) ___ cerca del río.* → *viven* — They live near the river.
» *Yo (hacer) ___ ejercicio todos los días.* → *hago* — I exercise every day.

## Extra Practice 2

Choose the correct form.

» *¿Ustedes (querer) ___ más pan?* → *quieren* — Do you want more bread?
» *Yo (venir) ___ en autobús.* → *vengo* — I come by bus.
» *Tú (decir) ___ la verdad.* → *dices* — You're telling the truth.
» *Vosotros (pedir) ___ demasiado.* → *pedís* — You ask for too much (no change).
