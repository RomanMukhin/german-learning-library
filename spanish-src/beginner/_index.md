This level gives you the grammar you need for everyday conversations: naming things, saying who does what, describing people and places, asking and answering questions, and talking about the present and your plans. The units build on each other, so it's best to work through them in order, but each one also stands on its own.

| You'll learn to... | Units |
|---|---|
| Name and describe things | Articles, Nouns, Adverbs, False Cognates |
| Say who does what | Subject Pronouns, Direct and Indirect Object Pronouns |
| Talk about now and the near future | Present, Present Progressive, Infinitives, Verbal Periphrasis |
| Describe, locate and identify | Ser vs. Estar |
| Build and link sentences | Asking Questions, Negation, Conjunctions and Contractions |
| Handle numbers | Cardinal Numbers, Telling Time |
| Read and pronounce | Alphabet and Syllables |

> [!hack] Learn nouns with their article
> Gender runs through everything in Spanish — articles, adjectives, pronouns. Write and say every new noun with *el* or *la* and you'll get agreement right much sooner.

> [!fact] Three verb families
> Every Spanish verb ends in *-ar*, *-er* or *-ir*. Once you know the present-tense endings for each family, you can use hundreds of regular verbs straight away.
