## Negation Using No

The simplest way to make a Spanish sentence negative is to put ***no*** directly **in front of the verb**. There is no helper like English *don't* or *doesn't*.

» *Hablo francés.* → *No hablo francés.* — I don't speak French.
» *Ella trabaja hoy.* → *Ella no trabaja hoy.* — She isn't working today.
» *Es caro.* → *No es caro.* — It isn't expensive.

### No and pronouns

If there are object or reflexive pronouns before the verb, *no* goes in front of them:

» *No lo sé.* — I don't know.
» *No me gusta.* — I don't like it.
» *No se lo digas.* — Don't tell him.

### Compound verbs

With *estar* + participle or *haber* + participle, *no* goes before the first (conjugated) verb:

» *No estoy durmiendo.* — I'm not sleeping.
» *No he terminado.* — I haven't finished.

### Answering no

When answering a question, you'll often hear *no* twice: once as the answer, once to negate the verb.

» *—¿Tienes frío? —No, no tengo frío.* — Are you cold? — No, I'm not cold.

> [!trap] No is not "not" after the verb
> ~~*Hablo no inglés.*~~ → *No hablo inglés.* — The *no* always goes before the verb and its pronouns.

### No in tags

*¿no?* at the end of a sentence asks for agreement: *Hace calor, ¿no?* — It's hot, isn't it?

## Negative Words and Phrases

Spanish has a full set of negative words, each with a positive partner.

| Positive | Negative |
|---|---|
| *algo* — something | *nada* — nothing |
| *alguien* — someone | *nadie* — no one |
| *alguno / algún, alguna* — some, any | *ninguno / ningún, ninguna* — none, no |
| *siempre* — always | *nunca, jamás* — never |
| *también* — also | *tampoco* — neither, not either |
| *o... o* — either... or | *ni... ni* — neither... nor |
| *ya* — already | *todavía no, aún no* — not yet |

### Double negatives are correct

When a negative word comes **after** the verb, you must also put *no* before the verb. In English this would be a double negative; in Spanish it's required.

» *No veo nada.* — I can't see anything. (literally "I don't see nothing")
» *No conozco a nadie aquí.* — I don't know anyone here.
» *No voy nunca al gimnasio.* — I never go to the gym.
» *No tengo ningún problema.* — I don't have any problem.

### Or put the negative word first

If the negative word goes **before** the verb, drop *no*:

» *Nunca voy al gimnasio.* — I never go to the gym.
» *Nadie sabe la respuesta.* — Nobody knows the answer.
» *Tampoco me gusta.* — I don't like it either.

### Ninguno

*ninguno* agrees with the noun and shortens to *ningún* before a masculine singular noun. It's almost always singular:

» *No hay ningún banco por aquí.* — There's no bank around here.
» *No tengo ninguna idea.* — I have no idea.
» *—¿Tienes hermanos? —No, ninguno.* — Do you have siblings? — No, none.

### Tampoco vs también

» *—Me gusta el jazz. —A mí también.* — I like jazz. — Me too.
» *—No me gusta el jazz. —A mí tampoco.* — I don't like jazz. — Me neither.

> [!trap] Never mix no with a preceding negative
> ~~*Nadie no vino.*~~ → *Nadie vino.* / *No vino nadie.* — Nobody came.

## Comprehensive Review 1

Make the sentences negative.

» *Tengo coche.* → *No tengo coche.* — I don't have a car.
» *Te entiendo.* → *No te entiendo.* — I don't understand you.
» *Siempre como carne.* → *Nunca como carne.* / *No como carne nunca.* — I never eat meat.
» *Hay algo en la caja.* → *No hay nada en la caja.* — There's nothing in the box.
» *Alguien llama.* → *Nadie llama.* / *No llama nadie.* — Nobody's calling.

## Comprehensive Review 2

Choose the right negative word.

» *No quiero ___.* (anything) → *nada*
» *___ viene a la reunión.* (nobody) → *Nadie*
» *No he visto ___ película de ese director.* (any) → *ninguna*
» *—Yo no fumo. —Yo ___.* (neither) → *tampoco*
» *No tengo ___ hermanos ___ hermanas.* → *ni... ni*
» *No he comido ___.* (yet) → *todavía* — *Todavía no he comido.* — I haven't eaten yet.

## Extra Practice 3

Spot and fix the mistakes.

» ~~*No tengo algo.*~~ → *No tengo nada.* — I don't have anything.
» ~~*No conozco alguien.*~~ → *No conozco a nadie.* — I don't know anyone.
» ~~*No hay ningunos problemas.*~~ → *No hay ningún problema.* — There's no problem.
» ~~*Nunca no bebo café.*~~ → *Nunca bebo café.* — I never drink coffee.
» ~~*—No me gusta. —A mí también.*~~ → *—A mí tampoco.* — Me neither.
