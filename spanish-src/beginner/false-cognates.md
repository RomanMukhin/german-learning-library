## Nouns

Thousands of Spanish words look like English ones and mean the same: *hospital*, *animal*, *chocolate*, *universidad*. These true cognates are a gift. But some lookalikes — **false cognates** or *falsos amigos* — mean something else entirely.

| Spanish | Actually means | Not | Which is |
|---|---|---|---|
| *la carpeta* | folder | carpet | *la alfombra* |
| *la librería* | bookshop | library | *la biblioteca* |
| *el éxito* | success | exit | *la salida* |
| *la ropa* | clothes | rope | *la cuerda* |
| *la lectura* | reading | lecture | *la conferencia* |
| *el pariente* | relative | parent | *el padre / la madre* |
| *la fábrica* | factory | fabric | *la tela* |
| *la sopa* | soup | soap | *el jabón* |
| *el collar* | necklace | collar | *el cuello* (of a shirt) |
| *la decepción* | disappointment | deception | *el engaño* |
| *el codo* | elbow | code | *el código* |
| *la casualidad* | chance, coincidence | casualty | *la víctima* |

» *Compré dos novelas en la librería.* — I bought two novels at the bookshop.
» *La obra fue un gran éxito.* — The play was a great success.
» *Tengo muchos parientes en México.* — I have lots of relatives in Mexico.
» *¡Qué casualidad verte aquí!* — What a coincidence to see you here!

> [!trap] Embarazada
> The most famous trap: *estar embarazada* means "to be pregnant". For "embarrassed", say *tener vergüenza* or *estar avergonzado/a*.

## Adjectives and Adverbs

| Spanish | Actually means | Not | Which is |
|---|---|---|---|
| *actual* | current, present-day | actual | *real, verdadero* |
| *actualmente* | currently, nowadays | actually | *en realidad* |
| *embarazada* | pregnant | embarrassed | *avergonzado/a* |
| *constipado* | having a cold | constipated | *estreñido* |
| *largo* | long | large | *grande* |
| *sensible* | sensitive | sensible | *sensato* |
| *simpático* | nice, friendly | sympathetic | *comprensivo, compasivo* |
| *excitado* | (sexually) aroused | excited | *emocionado, entusiasmado* |
| *eventualmente* | possibly, occasionally | eventually | *al final, finalmente* |
| *fastidioso* | annoying | fastidious | *meticuloso, quisquilloso* |

» *Actualmente vivo en Quito.* — I currently live in Quito.
» *En realidad, no me gusta el café.* — Actually, I don't like coffee.
» *Es un río muy largo.* — It's a very long river.
» *Mi hermano es muy sensible y llora con las películas.* — My brother is very sensitive and cries at films.
» *Estoy muy emocionada por el viaje.* — I'm really excited about the trip.

> [!hack] Actual means "now"
> Think of *actualidad* (current affairs). *El presidente actual* is the current president, not the "real" one.

## Verbs

| Spanish | Actually means | Not | Which is |
|---|---|---|---|
| *asistir (a)* | to attend | to assist | *ayudar* |
| *atender* | to serve, look after | to attend | *asistir a* |
| *realizar* | to carry out, achieve | to realise | *darse cuenta (de)* |
| *molestar* | to bother | to molest | *abusar de* |
| *recordar* | to remember, remind | to record | *grabar* |
| *introducir* | to insert, bring in | to introduce (a person) | *presentar* |
| *pretender* | to try, aim to | to pretend | *fingir* |
| *soportar* | to put up with | to support | *apoyar* |
| *contestar* | to answer | to contest | *impugnar, disputar* |
| *aplicar* | to apply (paint, a rule) | to apply (for a job) | *solicitar* |

» *Asisto a clases de baile los martes.* — I attend dance classes on Tuesdays.
» *Me di cuenta de que era tarde.* — I realised it was late.
» *No me molestes, estoy trabajando.* — Don't bother me, I'm working.
» *Te presento a mi amiga Laura.* — Let me introduce you to my friend Laura.
» *No soporto el ruido.* — I can't stand the noise.

> [!trap] Introducir a person
> ~~*Te introduzco a mi madre.*~~ → *Te presento a mi madre.* — This is my mother / Let me introduce my mother.

## Comprehensive Review 1

Choose the right word.

» *Voy a la ___ para estudiar.* (library) → *biblioteca*
» *Necesito una ___ para mis documentos.* (folder) → *carpeta*
» *Estoy ___; tengo tos y fiebre.* (have a cold) → *constipado/a* / *resfriado/a*
» *Mi profesora es muy ___.* (nice) → *simpática*
» *Quiero ___ a mi novio a mis padres.* (introduce) → *presentar*

## Comprehensive Review 2

Spot the false friend and fix the sentence.

» ~~*Estoy muy embarazado porque olvidé su nombre.*~~ → *Me da mucha vergüenza porque olvidé su nombre.* — I'm really embarrassed because I forgot her name.
» ~~*Actualmente, no sé la respuesta.*~~ (meaning "actually") → *En realidad, no sé la respuesta.*
» ~~*Busco la éxito.*~~ → *Busco la salida.* — I'm looking for the exit.
» ~~*Realicé que estaba solo.*~~ → *Me di cuenta de que estaba solo.* — I realised I was alone.
» ~~*Voy a aplicar para el trabajo.*~~ → *Voy a solicitar el trabajo.* — I'm going to apply for the job.

> [!hack] When in doubt, check
> If a Spanish word looks exactly like an English one but the sentence suddenly makes no sense, you've probably met a false friend. Look it up before you use it.
