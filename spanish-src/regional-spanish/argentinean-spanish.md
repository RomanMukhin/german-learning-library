## Pronunciation: sheísmo and intonation

Argentina and Uruguay speak **Rioplatense Spanish** (from the Río de la Plata region). Its best-known feature is the pronunciation of ***ll*** and ***y*** as /ʃ/ (like English *sh*) or /ʒ/ (like the *s* in *measure*):

» *calle* /ˈkaʃe/ — street
» *yo* /ʃo/ — I
» *me llamo* /me ˈʃamo/ — my name is

The intonation is strongly influenced by Italian, because millions of Italians emigrated to Argentina in the 19th and 20th centuries. Final *s* is often softened to an *h* sound: *más o menos* → *mah o menoh*.

## Grammar: voseo

Instead of *tú*, Argentines use ***vos*** for informal *you* (singular). *Vos* has its own present-tense and command forms, stressed on the last syllable:

| Verb | *tú* | *vos* | Command (*vos*) |
|---|---|---|---|
| *hablar* | *hablas* | *hablás* | *¡hablá!* |
| *comer* | *comes* | *comés* | *¡comé!* |
| *vivir* | *vives* | *vivís* | *¡viví!* |
| *ser* | *eres* | *sos* | *¡sé!* |
| *tener* | *tienes* | *tenés* (no stem change) | *¡tené!* |
| *venir* | *vienes* | *venís* | *¡vení!* |

» ***¿Vos sabés** dónde está?* — Do you know where it is?
» ***Vení** acá.* — Come here.
» *¿De dónde **sos**?* — Where are you from?

The object and possessive forms stay the same as for *tú*: *te*, *tu*, *contigo*: ***Vos** tenés **tu** libro.* Other tenses mostly use the normal *tú* forms: *vos comiste* (preterite).

> [!fact] Voseo is not only Argentinean
> *Vos* is also used in Uruguay, Paraguay, most of Central America and parts of Colombia, Venezuela and Chile, with slightly different verb forms.

## Vocabulary and lunfardo

**Lunfardo** is Buenos Aires slang, born in the late 1800s and strongly influenced by Italian. Many of its words are now everyday Argentine Spanish.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *che* | hey, mate (to attract attention) |
| *boludo / boluda* | idiot (insult) or "mate" between close friends |
| *laburo / laburar* | job / to work (from Italian *lavoro*) |
| *pibe / piba* | kid, young person |
| *mina* | woman, girl (informal) |
| *guita* | money |
| *quilombo* | mess, chaos |
| *re-* | very (*re lindo* — really nice) |
| *bárbaro* | great |
| *colectivo / bondi* | bus |
| *mate* | the national herbal drink, shared from one gourd |

» ***Che**, ¿vamos a tomar unos mates?* — Hey, shall we have some mate?
» *Este **laburo** es un **quilombo**.* — This job is a mess.
