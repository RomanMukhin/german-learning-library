Spanish is the official language of 20 countries, and about 500 million people speak it as a first language. Speakers from Madrid, Mexico City and Buenos Aires understand each other without trouble, but each region has its own accent, some grammar of its own and a lot of local vocabulary.

| Feature | Spain | Most of Latin America |
|---|---|---|
| plural *you* (informal) | *vosotros* | *ustedes* |
| *c* before *e/i*, and *z* | /θ/ (like English *th*) | /s/ |
| recent past | *He comido* (present perfect) | *Comí* (preterite), in many areas |
| car / computer | *coche*, *ordenador* | *carro / auto*, *computadora* |

> [!hack] Learn one variety, recognise the rest
> Pick one accent to imitate (the one you hear most), but learn to recognise the others. The grammar you learn in the other levels works everywhere; only the details on this page change.
