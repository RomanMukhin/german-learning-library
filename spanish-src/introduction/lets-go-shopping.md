## Clothing

*La ropa* (clothes) is a singular word in Spanish: *Esta ropa es nueva.*

### Tops and bottoms

| Spanish | English | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| *la camiseta* / *la playera* (Mex.) | T-shirt | *la camisa* | shirt |
| *la blusa* | blouse | *el suéter* / *el jersey* (Spain) | jumper, sweater |
| *la sudadera* | sweatshirt, hoodie | *el pantalón* / *los pantalones* | trousers |
| *los vaqueros* / *los jeans* | jeans | *el pantalón corto* / *el short* | shorts |
| *la falda* | skirt | *el vestido* | dress |
| *el traje* | suit | *la corbata* | tie |

### Colours

Colours are adjectives and go **after** the noun. Those ending in *-o* change for gender; the rest only for number.

*rojo* (red), *blanco* (white), *negro* (black), *amarillo* (yellow), *azul* (blue), *verde* (green), *gris* (grey), *marrón* / *café* (brown), *rosa* (pink), *naranja* (orange).

» *una camisa blanca* — a white shirt
» *los zapatos negros* — the black shoes
» *una falda azul* — a blue skirt

> [!trap] Colour after the noun
> ~~una roja chaqueta~~ → *una chaqueta roja*.

## Money Matters!

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *el dinero* / *la plata* (LatAm, informal) | money |
| *el precio* | price |
| *barato / barata* | cheap |
| *caro / cara* | expensive |
| *las rebajas* / *las ofertas* | the sales / special offers |
| *el descuento* | discount |
| *el efectivo* | cash |
| *la tarjeta (de crédito)* | (credit) card |
| *el cambio* / *la vuelta* | change |
| *el recibo* / *el tique* | receipt |

### Bigger numbers for prices

*diez* (10), *veinte* (20), *treinta* (30), *cincuenta* (50), *cien* (100), *ciento cincuenta* (150), *doscientos* (200), *quinientos* (500), *mil* (1,000).

» *¿Cuánto cuesta esta chaqueta? —Cuesta cincuenta euros.* — How much is this jacket? — It's fifty euros.
» *¿Cuánto cuestan estos zapatos? —Ochenta dólares.* — How much are these shoes? — Eighty dollars.
» *¡Qué caro! ¿Tiene algo más barato?* — How expensive! Do you have anything cheaper?
» *¿Hay descuento?* — Is there a discount?

> [!fact] Commas and points
> Many Spanish-speaking countries write prices with a decimal comma: *12,50 €* is twelve euros fifty. Thousands may use a point: *1.000*.

> [!trap] cien vs. ciento
> Exactly 100 is *cien*: *cien pesos*. Above it, use *ciento*: *ciento veinte pesos* (120).

## At the Store

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *¿En qué le puedo ayudar?* | How can I help you? |
| *Solo estoy mirando, gracias.* | I'm just looking, thanks. |
| *Busco un vestido para una boda.* | I'm looking for a dress for a wedding. |
| *¿Qué talla tiene?* | What size are you? (clothes) |
| *¿Qué número calza?* | What size are you? (shoes) |
| *¿Lo tiene en azul?* | Do you have it in blue? |
| *¿Me lo puedo probar?* | Can I try it on? |
| *¿Dónde están los probadores?* | Where are the fitting rooms? |
| *Me queda grande / pequeño.* | It's too big / small for me. |
| *Me queda bien.* | It fits well. |
| *Me lo llevo.* | I'll take it. |
| *¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?* | Can I pay by card? |

Use ***este / esta*** (this) and ***estos / estas*** (these) to point to items:

» *¿Me puedo probar esta camisa?* — Can I try on this shirt?
» *Estos pantalones me quedan pequeños.* — These trousers are too small for me.
» *Me gusta este abrigo. Me lo llevo.* — I like this coat. I'll take it.

> [!hack] Lo, la, los, las
> In *Me lo llevo* the *lo* means "it" and matches the item: *la falda → me la llevo*; *los zapatos → me los llevo*.
