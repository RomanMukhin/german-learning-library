## Let's Have a Party!

*La fiesta* is the general word for a party. Some common kinds:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *la fiesta de cumpleaños* | birthday party |
| *la fiesta sorpresa* | surprise party |
| *la fiesta de inauguración* / *de estreno* | housewarming |
| *la cena* / *la velada* | dinner party |
| *el cóctel* | cocktail party |
| *la boda* / *el banquete de bodas* | wedding / wedding reception |
| *el bautizo* | christening |
| *la despedida de soltera / soltero* | hen / stag party |
| *el baby shower* | baby shower |
| *la quinceañera* | 15th birthday celebration |
| *la pachanga*, *el carrete* (Chile), *el rumbón* | house party (informal) |

To throw a party is *dar una fiesta* or *hacer una fiesta*.

### Planning with ir a

To talk about plans, use ***ir a*** + infinitive — "going to":

| *ir* | |
|---|---|
| *voy a* | I'm going to |
| *vas a* | you're going to |
| *va a* | he / she is going to |
| *vamos a* | we're going to |
| *van a* | they / you all are going to |

» *Vamos a dar una fiesta el sábado.* — We're going to have a party on Saturday.
» *¿Vas a invitar a Laura?* — Are you going to invite Laura?
» *¡Vamos a celebrar!* — Let's celebrate!

> [!hack] Vamos a = "let's"
> *Vamos a bailar* can mean both "we're going to dance" and "let's dance!". Tone tells you which.

## What Do We Need?

### Getting ready

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *planear* / *organizar* | to plan / to organise |
| *mandar las invitaciones* | to send the invitations |
| *ir de compras* | to go shopping |
| *preparar la comida* | to prepare the food |
| *decorar* | to decorate |

Say what has to be done with ***tener que*** + infinitive (to have to) and ***necesitar*** (to need):

» *Tenemos que comprar bebidas.* — We have to buy drinks.
» *Tienes que mandar las invitaciones hoy.* — You have to send the invitations today.
» *Necesitamos globos y velas.* — We need balloons and candles.

### Party things and people

| Spanish | English | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| *los adornos* | decorations | *el globo* | balloon |
| *las serpentinas* | streamers | *la vela* | candle |
| *el pastel* / *la tarta* / *la torta* | cake | *el helado* | ice cream |
| *los caramelos* / *los dulces* | sweets | *las bebidas* | drinks |
| *el regalo* | present | *el lazo* | bow, ribbon |
| *los invitados* | guests | *el anfitrión / la anfitriona* | host |
| *los parientes* | relatives | *el / la aguafiestas* | party pooper |

> [!fact] The cake has many names
> *Pastel* is common in Mexico, *tarta* in Spain and *torta* in much of South America. All of them will get you cake.

## We're Ready to Party!

### Things to do

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *bailar* | to dance |
| *comer* y *beber* / *tomar* | to eat and drink |
| *escuchar música* | to listen to music |
| *abrir los regalos* | to open the presents |
| *soplar las velas* | to blow out the candles |
| *hacer un brindis* | to make a toast |
| *jugar juegos* | to play games |
| *romper la piñata* | to break the piñata |
| *sacar fotos* | to take photos |

### Giving the details

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *¿Dónde es la fiesta?* | Where's the party? |
| *La fiesta es en mi casa / en el parque.* | The party is at my house / in the park. |
| *La fiesta es en casa de Marta.* | The party is at Marta's. |
| *¿Cuándo es?* — *Es el viernes.* | When is it? — It's on Friday. |
| *¿A qué hora empieza?* | What time does it start? |
| *Empieza a las ocho.* | It starts at eight. |

» *La fiesta sorpresa es el sábado a las siete. ¡No llegues tarde!* — The surprise party is on Saturday at seven. Don't be late!
» *¡Salud!* — Cheers!

> [!trap] Events use ser for place
> For where an **event** takes place, use *ser*: *La fiesta **es** en mi casa*. For where a **thing or person** is, use *estar*: *Mi casa **está** cerca del parque.*

> [!fact] La piñata
> At children's parties in Mexico and elsewhere, blindfolded kids take turns hitting a *piñata* filled with sweets while everyone sings *Dale, dale, dale…*
