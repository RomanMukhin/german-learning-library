## Introduction

This unit puts together the first building blocks of a café visit: a greeting, a quick "how are you?" and a drink order.

### Core words

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *sí* | yes |
| *no* | no |
| *un café* | a coffee |
| *agua* | water |
| *cómo* | how |
| *qué* | what |
| *quiero* | I want |
| *quieres* | you want |

### Accents matter

These words carry a written accent; don't leave it out:

» *sí* (yes) vs. *si* (if)
» *café* — stress on the last syllable: ca-FÉ
» *cómo* and *qué* — accents mark question words
» *días* (in *Buenos días*) and *estás* (in *¿Cómo estás?*)

### Two-sided question marks

Spanish questions open with *¿* and close with *?*:

» *¿Qué quieres?* — What do you want?
» *¿Quieres agua?* — Do you want water?

> [!trap] Silent u in qu
> In *quiero*, *quieres* and *qué*, the *qu* sounds like English *k*: "KYEH-ro", not ~~"KWEH-ro"~~.

## Good Afternoon

Greetings change with the time of day:

| Spanish | When | English |
|---|---|---|
| *Buenos días.* | morning, until lunch | Good morning. |
| *Buenas tardes.* | after lunch until dark | Good afternoon / evening. |
| *Buenas noches.* | after dark, also as goodbye | Good evening / good night. |

Note *buenos* with *días* (masculine) but *buenas* with *tardes* and *noches* (feminine).

### How are you?

Feelings and conditions use ***estar***:

| | *estar* |
|---|---|
| *(yo) estoy* | I am |
| *(tú) estás* | you are |

» *¿Cómo estás?* — How are you?
» *Estoy bien.* — I'm fine.
» *Estoy mal.* — I'm not well.
» *Estoy muy bien, gracias.* — I'm very well, thanks.

> [!fact] When is it "tarde"?
> In Spain lunch is late, around 2 or 3 p.m., so people say *Buenos días* until then and *Buenas tardes* until 8 or 9 p.m. In much of Latin America the switch happens around noon.

## Meet Up at the Café!

Now order a drink. Use ***querer*** (to want):

| | *querer* |
|---|---|
| *(yo) quiero* | I want |
| *(tú) quieres* | you want |

» *¿Qué quieres?* — What do you want?
» *Quiero un café.* — I want a coffee.
» *Quiero agua.* — I want water.
» *¿Quieres un café? —Sí, gracias.* — Do you want a coffee? — Yes, thanks.
» *¿Quieres agua? —No, quiero un café.* — Do you want water? — No, I want a coffee.

### A café dialogue

» *—¡Buenas tardes, Elena! ¿Cómo estás?* — Good afternoon, Elena! How are you?
» *—Estoy bien. ¿Y tú?* — I'm fine. And you?
» *—Muy bien. ¿Qué quieres?* — Very well. What would you like?
» *—Quiero un café, por favor.* — I'd like a coffee, please.

### Ways to order coffee

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| *un café solo* / *un expreso* | an espresso |
| *un café con leche* | a white coffee |
| *un cortado* | an espresso with a dash of milk |
| *un café americano* | an americano |
| *un café de olla* (Mex.) | spiced coffee with cinnamon |

> [!hack] Agua con gas o sin gas
> Ordering water, you may be asked *¿Con gas o sin gas?* — sparkling or still?
