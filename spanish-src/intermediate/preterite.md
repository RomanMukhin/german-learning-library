## Regular AR

The **preterite** describes completed actions at a definite moment in the past: *Ayer hablé con Ana.* — Yesterday I spoke to Ana.

Regular *-ar* verbs drop *-ar* and add these endings:

| Subject | Ending | *hablar* | *trabajar* |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *-é* | *hablé* | *trabajé* |
| *tú* | *-aste* | *hablaste* | *trabajaste* |
| *él / ella / usted* | *-ó* | *habló* | *trabajó* |
| *nosotros* | *-amos* | *hablamos* | *trabajamos* |
| *vosotros* | *-asteis* | *hablasteis* | *trabajasteis* |
| *ellos / ustedes* | *-aron* | *hablaron* | *trabajaron* |

### Watch the accents

The written accents on *yo* and *él* change the meaning completely:

» *hablo* — I speak (present) vs. *habló* — he spoke (preterite)
» *trabaje* (subjunctive) vs. *trabajé* — I worked

### *Nosotros* is the same as the present

*Hablamos* can mean "we speak" or "we spoke". Time words decide: *Ayer hablamos* (past), *Siempre hablamos* (present).

» *Compré pan esta mañana.* — I bought bread this morning.
» *¿A qué hora llegaste?* — What time did you arrive?
» *Ellos bailaron toda la noche.* — They danced all night.

> [!trap] -ar verbs never change their stem
> Stem-changing *-ar* verbs in the present (*pensar → pienso*) are regular in the preterite: *pensé, pensaste, pensó*.

## Regular ER/IR

*-er* and *-ir* verbs share one set of endings:

| Subject | Ending | *comer* | *vivir* |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *-í* | *comí* | *viví* |
| *tú* | *-iste* | *comiste* | *viviste* |
| *él / ella / usted* | *-ió* | *comió* | *vivió* |
| *nosotros* | *-imos* | *comimos* | *vivimos* |
| *vosotros* | *-isteis* | *comisteis* | *vivisteis* |
| *ellos / ustedes* | *-ieron* | *comieron* | *vivieron* |

» *Anoche comimos en un restaurante peruano.* — Last night we ate at a Peruvian restaurant.
» *Mi abuela vivió en Cuba diez años.* — My grandmother lived in Cuba for ten years.
» *¿Recibiste mi mensaje?* — Did you get my message?

For *-ir* verbs, *nosotros* again matches the present (*vivimos*). For *-er* verbs it doesn't: present *comemos*, preterite *comimos*.

> [!hack] Look for the í
> In the *-er/-ir* preterite, every form has an *i*: *-í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron*.

## Extra Practice with AR/ER/IR Verbs

Conjugate in the preterite:

» *yo / cocinar* → *cociné*
» *tú / beber* → *bebiste*
» *ella / escribir* → *escribió*
» *nosotros / aprender* → *aprendimos*
» *vosotros / caminar* → *caminasteis*
» *ellos / abrir* → *abrieron*
» *usted / vender* → *vendió*
» *yo / salir* → *salí*

Put the sentences into the past:

» *Hoy estudio dos horas.* → *Ayer estudié dos horas.*
» *Siempre comen a las dos.* → *Ayer comieron a las dos.*
» *Escribimos una carta.* → *Escribimos una carta* (same form; add *ayer* or *anoche*).

Questions with the preterite:

» *¿Qué compraste?* — What did you buy?
» *¿Cuándo volvieron?* — When did they come back?

> [!trap] Don't add "did"
> Spanish has no helper verb for past questions or negatives: ~~¿Hizo comprar?~~ → *¿Compró?*; *No llamé.* — I didn't call.

## Spelling Changes

Some verbs change their spelling in the **yo** form only, to keep the sound of the last consonant before *-é*:

| Ending | Change | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *-car* | *c → qu* | *buscar → busqué*, *tocar → toqué*, *sacar → saqué* |
| *-gar* | *g → gu* | *llegar → llegué*, *pagar → pagué*, *jugar → jugué* |
| *-zar* | *z → c* | *empezar → empecé*, *almorzar → almorcé*, *cruzar → crucé* |

The other forms are regular: *buscaste, buscó, buscamos...*

» *Busqué mis llaves por todas partes.* — I looked everywhere for my keys.
» *Llegué tarde al trabajo.* — I got to work late.
» *Empecé un curso nuevo.* — I started a new course.

> [!fact] Why it happens
> *c* before *e* sounds like *s* or *th*, and *g* before *e* sounds like an *h*. Adding *u* keeps the hard *k* and *g* sounds, and *z* is simply written *c* before *e* in Spanish.

## More on Spelling Changes

### *i* becomes *y*

When an *-er* or *-ir* stem ends in a vowel, the *i* of the third-person endings turns into *y*: *-ió → -yó*, *-ieron → -yeron*.

| Verb | *él / ella* | *ellos* | other forms |
|---|---|---|---|
| *leer* | *leyó* | *leyeron* | *leí, leíste, leímos* |
| *creer* | *creyó* | *creyeron* | *creí, creíste* |
| *oír* | *oyó* | *oyeron* | *oí, oíste* |
| *caer* | *cayó* | *cayeron* | *caí, caíste* |
| *construir* | *construyó* | *construyeron* | *construí, construiste* |

Notice the accent on *í* in the other forms of *leer, creer, oír, caer* (*leíste, leímos*), but not in *-uir* verbs (*construiste, construimos*).

» *¿Leyeron el periódico?* — Did they read the newspaper?
» *El niño se cayó.* — The child fell over.
» *Oyó un ruido extraño.* — She heard a strange noise.

Other *-uir* verbs: *incluir, destruir, huir, contribuir, distribuir*.

> [!trap] Not for traer
> *Traer* is irregular (*trajo, trajeron*), so it does not follow this rule.

## Irregulars

Many common verbs have an irregular stem in the preterite and take a special set of endings **without accents**: *-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron*.

| Infinitive | Stem | *yo* | *él* |
|---|---|---|---|
| *tener* | *tuv-* | *tuve* | *tuvo* |
| *estar* | *estuv-* | *estuve* | *estuvo* |
| *andar* | *anduv-* | *anduve* | *anduvo* |
| *poder* | *pud-* | *pude* | *pudo* |
| *poner* | *pus-* | *puse* | *puso* |
| *saber* | *sup-* | *supe* | *supo* |
| *haber* | *hub-* | *hube* | *hubo* (there was) |
| *venir* | *vin-* | *vine* | *vino* |
| *querer* | *quis-* | *quise* | *quiso* |
| *hacer* | *hic-* | *hice* | *hizo* (c → z) |

### *j* stems: *-eron*, not *-ieron*

*decir → dije, dijiste, dijo, dijimos, dijisteis, dijeron*; also *traer → traje*, *conducir → conduje*, *producir → produje*.

### *Ser* and *ir*: identical

*fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron* means both "I was" and "I went". Context decides: *Fue a Roma* (went) / *Fue un buen día* (was).

### *Dar* and *ver*

*dar → di, diste, dio, dimos, disteis, dieron* (takes *-er/-ir* endings); *ver → vi, viste, vio, vimos, visteis, vieron*. No accents.

» *Hubo un accidente.* — There was an accident.
» *Me dijeron la verdad.* — They told me the truth.

## Stem-Changing Verbs

*-ar* and *-er* verbs never change their stem in the preterite. But **-ir verbs** that change their stem in the present also change in the preterite, only in the **third person** (singular and plural), and only one step:

| Present change | Preterite change | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *e → ie* / *e → i* | *e → i* | *preferir → prefirió, prefirieron* |
| *o → ue* | *o → u* | *dormir → durmió, durmieron* |

| Subject | *pedir* | *dormir* |
|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *pedí* | *dormí* |
| *tú* | *pediste* | *dormiste* |
| *él / ella / usted* | *pidió* | *durmió* |
| *nosotros* | *pedimos* | *dormimos* |
| *vosotros* | *pedisteis* | *dormisteis* |
| *ellos / ustedes* | *pidieron* | *durmieron* |

» *El niño durmió diez horas.* — The child slept for ten hours.
» *Pidieron una pizza.* — They ordered a pizza.

> [!hack] The "boot" shrinks to a sandal
> In the present the change hits four forms. In the preterite it only hits *él* and *ellos*.

## More on Stem-Changing Verbs

Common *-ir* stem-changers you will meet in the preterite:

| *e → i* | *o → u* |
|---|---|
| *pedir → pidió* | *dormir → durmió* |
| *servir → sirvió* | *morir → murió* |
| *repetir → repitió* | |
| *seguir → siguió* | |
| *sentir → sintió* | |
| *mentir → mintió* | |
| *preferir → prefirió* | |
| *divertirse → se divirtió* | |
| *vestirse → se vistió* | |

» *El camarero nos sirvió la sopa.* — The waiter served us the soup.
» *Mi abuelo murió en 2010.* — My grandfather died in 2010.
» *¿Os divertisteis?* — Did you have fun? (*vosotros* doesn't change)
» *Se sintieron mal.* — They felt ill.

Verbs ending in *-eír* combine the change with dropped letters: *reír → rio, rieron*; *sonreír → sonrió, sonrieron*. (*Rio* has no accent under current spelling rules, though you will often see *rió*.)

> [!trap] -ar and -er stay regular
> ~~pienzó~~, ~~vuelvió~~ → *pensó*, *volvió*. Only *-ir* verbs change.

## Comprehensive Review

| Type | Example (*yo / él / ellos*) |
|---|---|
| regular *-ar* | *canté / cantó / cantaron* |
| regular *-er/-ir* | *bebí / bebió / bebieron* |
| *-car, -gar, -zar* | *saqué / sacó / sacaron* |
| *i → y* | *leí / leyó / leyeron* |
| irregular stem | *tuve / tuvo / tuvieron* |
| *j* stem | *dije / dijo / dijeron* |
| *-ir* stem change | *pedí / pidió / pidieron* |
| *ser / ir* | *fui / fue / fueron* |

The preterite is used for: completed events (*Terminé el libro*), beginnings and ends (*La película empezó a las ocho*), events at a specific time (*En 1999 nos casamos*) and sequences (*Me levanté, me duché y salí*).

» *¿Qué hiciste el fin de semana?* — *Fui a la playa y jugué al voleibol.*
