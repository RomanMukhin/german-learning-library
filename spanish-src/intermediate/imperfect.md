## Regular Verbs

The **imperfect** is a past tense for things that were ongoing, repeated or simply the background of a story. English usually translates it as "used to", "was ...ing" or the simple past.

There are only two sets of endings: one for *-ar* verbs and one shared by *-er* and *-ir* verbs.

| Subject | *-ar*: *hablar* | *-er*: *comer* | *-ir*: *vivir* |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *hablaba* | *comía* | *vivía* |
| *tú* | *hablabas* | *comías* | *vivías* |
| *él / ella / usted* | *hablaba* | *comía* | *vivía* |
| *nosotros* | *hablábamos* | *comíamos* | *vivíamos* |
| *vosotros* | *hablabais* | *comíais* | *vivíais* |
| *ellos / ustedes* | *hablaban* | *comían* | *vivían* |

Things to notice:

- The *yo* and *él/ella/usted* forms are identical. Add a subject if needed: *Yo trabajaba, pero ella estudiaba.*
- *-ar* verbs only have an accent in the *nosotros* form (*hablábamos*); *-er/-ir* verbs have one on every *í*.
- There are **no stem changes** in the imperfect: *pensar → pensaba*, *dormir → dormía*, *pedir → pedía*.

» *Mi abuelo trabajaba en una fábrica.* — My grandfather worked in a factory.
» *¿Qué hacías?* — What were you doing?
» *Vivíamos cerca del mar.* — We used to live near the sea.

> [!hack] Hay → había
> The imperfect of *hay* (there is / are) is *había*, always singular: *Había muchos niños en el parque.*

## Irregular Verbs

Only **three** verbs are irregular in the imperfect, which makes it one of the friendliest tenses in Spanish.

| Subject | *ir* | *ser* | *ver* |
|---|---|---|---|
| *yo* | *iba* | *era* | *veía* |
| *tú* | *ibas* | *eras* | *veías* |
| *él / ella / usted* | *iba* | *era* | *veía* |
| *nosotros* | *íbamos* | *éramos* | *veíamos* |
| *vosotros* | *ibais* | *erais* | *veíais* |
| *ellos / ustedes* | *iban* | *eran* | *veían* |

*Ver* is only slightly irregular: it keeps the *e* of its stem (*ve- + ía*).

» *Cada domingo íbamos a misa.* — Every Sunday we went to mass.
» *Mi profesora era muy estricta.* — My teacher was very strict.
» *De pequeño, veía dibujos todas las mañanas.* — As a kid, I watched cartoons every morning.
» *Eran las cinco de la tarde.* — It was five in the afternoon.

Compounds of *ver* follow it: *prever → preveía*.

> [!trap] Iba a + infinitive
> *Iba a* + infinitive means "was going to": *Iba a llamarte, pero me dormí.* — I was going to call you, but I fell asleep.

## Uses

Use the imperfect for:

### 1. Habits and repeated actions

» *Siempre desayunábamos juntos.* — We always had breakfast together.
» *Los veranos íbamos al pueblo.* — In summer we used to go to the village.

Clue words: *siempre, a menudo, cada día, todos los años, normalmente, de niño.*

### 2. Actions in progress

» *Leía mientras mi hermano cocinaba.* — I was reading while my brother was cooking.

### 3. Times and dates

» *Era la una.* — It was one o'clock. *Eran las once.* — It was eleven.

### 4. Age

» *Cuando tenía diez años, aprendí a nadar.* — When I was ten, I learned to swim.

### 5. Descriptions, conditions and feelings

» *La casa era grande y tenía un jardín.* — The house was big and had a garden.
» *Estaba cansada y tenía hambre.* — She was tired and hungry.
» *No sabíamos la verdad.* — We didn't know the truth.

### 6. Background for another event

The imperfect sets the scene; the preterite interrupts it: *Llovía cuando salí.* — It was raining when I went out.

> [!fact] An "unfinished" past
> *Imperfect* means "not completed". The tense views an action from inside, without caring about its start or end.

## Comprehensive Review 1

| | Regular *-ar* | Regular *-er / -ir* | Irregular |
|---|---|---|---|
| Endings | *-aba, -abas, -aba, -ábamos, -abais, -aban* | *-ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían* | *ir, ser, ver* |

Conjugate:

» *yo / cantar* → *cantaba*
» *nosotros / tener* → *teníamos*
» *ellos / salir* → *salían*
» *tú / ser* → *eras*
» *vosotros / ir* → *ibais*
» *ella / ver* → *veía*

## Comprehensive Review 2

Identify the use of the imperfect in each sentence:

» *Mi perro se llamaba Toby.* → description
» *Eran las ocho.* → time
» *Todos los días caminaba al trabajo.* → habit
» *Mi madre tenía treinta años.* → age
» *Estábamos muy contentos.* → feeling / condition
» *Mientras ella dormía, yo trabajaba.* → actions in progress

## Comprehensive Review 3

Complete the story with the imperfect:

» *Cuando (ser) niña, (vivir) en un pueblo pequeño.* → *era, vivía*
» *Mi casa (tener) un patio enorme.* → *tenía*
» *Todos los veranos mis primos (venir) a visitarnos.* → *venían*
» *Nosotros (jugar) en la calle hasta muy tarde.* → *jugábamos*
» *(Hacer) mucho calor y (haber) muchos mosquitos.* → *Hacía, había*

## Comprehensive Review 4

Translate:

» I used to play the piano. → *Tocaba el piano.*
» We were watching TV. → *Veíamos la tele* (or *Estábamos viendo la tele*).
» It was two o'clock. → *Eran las dos.*
» My grandparents were very kind. → *Mis abuelos eran muy amables.*
» She was going to travel. → *Iba a viajar.*

> [!hack] Estaba + gerund
> For an action in progress you can also use the **past progressive**: *estaba* + gerund (*estaba leyendo*). It stresses the "right then" feeling.

## Extra Practice 3

Focus: describing the past. Turn the present into the imperfect.

» *Hace frío y nieva.* → *Hacía frío y nevaba.*
» *Mi hermano tiene doce años.* → *Mi hermano tenía doce años.*
» *Siempre comemos a las dos.* → *Siempre comíamos a las dos.*
» *Hay un parque cerca de casa.* → *Había un parque cerca de casa.*

> [!trap] No preterite clue words
> Words like *ayer, una vez* or *de repente* usually signal the **preterite**, not the imperfect. See [Preterite vs. Imperfect](intermediate/preterite-vs-imperfect).
