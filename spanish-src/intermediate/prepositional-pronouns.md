## Introduction

After a preposition (*para, de, sin, sobre, a, por, hacia...*), Spanish uses **prepositional pronouns**. Most look exactly like the subject pronouns; only the first two persons are different.

| Subject | Prepositional |
|---|---|
| *yo* | *mí* |
| *tú* | *ti* |
| *él / ella / usted* | *él / ella / usted* |
| *nosotros/as* | *nosotros/as* |
| *vosotros/as* | *vosotros/as* |
| *ellos / ellas / ustedes* | *ellos / ellas / ustedes* |

» *Este regalo es para ti.* — This present is for you.
» *¿Vienes sin mí?* — Are you coming without me?
» *Hablamos de ella.* — We talked about her.
» *Lo hice por ustedes.* — I did it for you all.
» *Detrás de nosotros había un perro.* — Behind us there was a dog.

### Accents

*Mí* has an accent to distinguish it from the possessive *mi* (my). *Ti* never has an accent, because there's no other *ti* to confuse it with.

» *Es para mí.* — It's for me. / *Es mi libro.* — It's my book.
» ~~para tí~~ → *para ti*

### With *a* for emphasis

*A* + prepositional pronoun often doubles an object pronoun for emphasis or clarity: *A mí me gusta.* *Le di el libro a ella.*

> [!trap] Él vs. el
> The pronoun *él* (he/him) keeps its accent after prepositions and doesn't contract: *de él, a él*, not ~~del~~ or ~~al~~.

## Exceptions

### *Con*: *conmigo, contigo, consigo*

With *con*, the first and second singular persons fuse into one word:

| | |
|---|---|
| *con* + *mí* | *conmigo* — with me |
| *con* + *ti* | *contigo* — with you |
| *con* + reflexive *sí* | *consigo* — with himself / herself / themselves |

» *¿Quieres bailar conmigo?* — Do you want to dance with me?
» *Voy contigo.* — I'll go with you.
» *Siempre lleva el móvil consigo.* — He always carries his phone with him.

The other persons are regular: *con él, con ella, con nosotros, con ustedes*.

### Reflexive *sí*

*Sí* (with an accent) is the reflexive prepositional pronoun for third persons: "himself, herself, themselves". It's often reinforced with *mismo*:

» *Habla mucho de sí mismo.* — He talks a lot about himself.
» *Lo quiere para sí.* — She wants it for herself.

### Prepositions that take *yo* and *tú*

A few prepositions use the subject forms *yo* and *tú* instead of *mí, ti*:

| Preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| *entre* | *entre tú y yo* — between you and me |
| *según* | *según tú* — according to you |
| *excepto, salvo, menos* | *todos menos yo* — everyone except me |
| *incluso* | *incluso tú* — even you |
| *como* | *alto como yo* — as tall as me |

> [!trap] Entre tú y yo
> ~~entre ti y mí~~ → *entre tú y yo*. English "between you and me" leads many learners astray.

## Comprehensive Review 1

Fill in the pronoun:

» *¿Es para ___ (me)?* → *mí*
» *No puedo vivir sin ___ (you, tú).* → *ti*
» *Vamos al cine con ___ (them, f.).* → *ellas*
» *¿Quieres venir ___ (with me)?* → *conmigo*
» *Siempre pienso en ___ (you, usted).* → *usted*
» *Según ___ (you, tú), ¿quién tiene razón?* → *tú*

## Comprehensive Review 2

Fix the errors:

» ~~Esto es para yo.~~ → *Esto es para mí.*
» ~~Voy con ti.~~ → *Voy contigo.*
» ~~Hablamos del.~~ → *Hablamos de él.*
» ~~Entre mí y ti.~~ → *Entre tú y yo.*
» ~~Lo hizo para tí.~~ → *Lo hizo para ti.*

Translate:

» She always takes her dog with her. → *Siempre lleva a su perro consigo.*
» Everyone came except me. → *Vinieron todos excepto yo.*

## Extra Practice 1

Focus: the basic forms.

» *Tengo una sorpresa para ___ (you all, ustedes).* → *ustedes*
» *Detrás de ___ (me) hay alguien.* → *mí*
» *Hablaban de ___ (us).* → *nosotros*
» *Confío en ___ (you, tú).* → *ti*
» *Esta carta es para ___ (him).* → *él*

## Extra Practice 2

Focus: *conmigo, contigo, consigo*.

» *¿Quién viene ___ (with me)?* → *conmigo*
» *Quiero hablar ___ (with you, tú).* → *contigo*
» *El profesor trajo los exámenes ___ (with him).* → *consigo*
» *Voy a la playa ___ (with them).* → *con ellos*

## Extra Practice 3

Focus: exceptions with *entre, según, menos, como*.

» *Esto queda entre ___ y ___ (you and me).* → *tú, yo*
» *Todos aprobaron menos ___ (you, tú).* → *tú*
» *Nadie canta como ___ (her).* → *ella*
» *Según ___ (me), es mejor esperar.* → *yo*

> [!hack] Hear it
> Say *conmigo, contigo* until they sound natural; they're among the most frequent words in Spanish songs.
