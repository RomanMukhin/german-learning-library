## Common Expressions

*Tener* means "to have", but in many fixed expressions English uses "to be". Literally, Spanish speakers **have** hunger, cold or fear.

First, a reminder of the present tense of *tener*: *tengo, tienes, tiene, tenemos, tenéis, tienen*.

| Expression | Literally | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *tener hambre* | to have hunger | to be hungry |
| *tener sed* | to have thirst | to be thirsty |
| *tener frío* | to have cold | to be cold |
| *tener calor* | to have heat | to be hot |
| *tener sueño* | to have sleepiness | to be sleepy |
| *tener miedo (de / a)* | to have fear | to be afraid (of) |
| *tener prisa* | to have hurry | to be in a hurry |
| *tener razón* | to have reason | to be right |
| *no tener razón* | | to be wrong |
| *tener ... años* | to have ... years | to be ... years old |
| *tener cuidado* | to have care | to be careful |
| *tener suerte* | to have luck | to be lucky |

» *Tengo mucha hambre.* — I'm very hungry.
» *¿Tienes frío?* — Are you cold?
» *Mi hijo tiene ocho años.* — My son is eight.
» *Tienes razón.* — You're right.
» *Ten cuidado con el perro.* — Be careful with the dog.

### Intensifying

Because *hambre, sed, frío* etc. are nouns, you intensify them with *mucho / mucha*, not *muy*:

» ~~Tengo muy hambre~~ → *Tengo mucha hambre* (feminine noun)
» *Tenemos mucho frío.* — We're very cold.

> [!trap] People vs. weather vs. things
> People **have** cold: *Tengo frío*. The weather **makes** cold: *Hace frío*. Objects **are** cold: *La sopa está fría*.

## Even More Expressions

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *tener que* + infinitive | to have to |
| *tener ganas de* + infinitive / noun | to feel like |
| *tener celos* | to be jealous |
| *tener vergüenza* | to be embarrassed / ashamed |
| *tener éxito* | to be successful |
| *tener la culpa (de)* | to be to blame (for) |
| *tener en cuenta* | to take into account |
| *tener lugar* | to take place |
| *tener dolor de* + body part | to have a ...ache |
| *tener fiebre* | to have a temperature |
| *tener ... de largo / ancho* | to be ... long / wide |
| *tener sentido* | to make sense |

### *Tener que*

*Tener que* + infinitive expresses obligation: "have to".

» *Tengo que trabajar mañana.* — I have to work tomorrow.
» *Tuvimos que esperar dos horas.* — We had to wait two hours.
» *No tienes que venir.* — You don't have to come.

### *Tener ganas de*

» *Tengo ganas de verte.* — I'm looking forward to seeing you / I feel like seeing you.
» *No tengo ganas de cocinar.* — I don't feel like cooking.
» *¿Tienes ganas de un helado?* — Do you fancy an ice cream?

### Others in context

» *La reunión tendrá lugar el martes.* — The meeting will take place on Tuesday.
» *Tengo dolor de cabeza.* — I have a headache.
» *Eso no tiene sentido.* — That doesn't make sense.
» *¿Quién tiene la culpa?* — Who's to blame?

> [!hack] Hay que vs. tener que
> *Tener que* is personal (*Tengo que estudiar* — I have to study). *Hay que* is impersonal (*Hay que estudiar* — One has to study).

## Comprehensive Review

Choose the expression:

» It's 40 degrees: *Tengo ___.* → *calor*
» You haven't eaten all day: *Tienes ___.* → *hambre*
» You see a big spider: *Tengo ___.* → *miedo*
» Your train leaves in five minutes: *Tengo ___.* → *prisa*
» You won the lottery: *¡Qué ___ tienes!* → *suerte*
» Your friend said Paris is the capital of France: *Tienes ___.* → *razón*

Translate:

» How old are you? → *¿Cuántos años tienes?*
» We have to leave. → *Tenemos que irnos.*
» I don't feel like going out. → *No tengo ganas de salir.*
» She's very thirsty. → *Tiene mucha sed.*

## Extra Practice 1

Focus: physical feelings.

» *Abre la ventana, ___ (we're hot).* → *tenemos calor*
» *Los niños ___ (are sleepy).* → *tienen sueño*
» *Ponte el abrigo si ___ (you're cold).* → *tienes frío*
» *¿___ (are you thirsty, usted)?* → *¿Tiene sed?*

## Extra Practice 2

Focus: *tener que* and *tener ganas de* in different tenses.

» *Ayer ___ (I had to) trabajar hasta tarde.* → *tuve que*
» *Mañana ___ (we will have to) madrugar.* → *tendremos que*
» *De niño siempre ___ (I felt like) jugar.* → *tenía ganas de*
» *¿___ (do you feel like) ir al cine?* → *¿Tienes ganas de*

## Extra Practice 3

Focus: less common expressions.

» *El concierto ___ (took place) en el estadio.* → *tuvo lugar*
» *No es mi culpa; tú ___ (are to blame).* → *tienes la culpa*
» *Su empresa ___ (is very successful).* → *tiene mucho éxito*
» *La mesa ___ (is two metres long).* → *tiene dos metros de largo*

> [!trap] Age takes tener
> ~~Soy 20 años~~ → *Tengo 20 años*. Age always takes *tener*.
