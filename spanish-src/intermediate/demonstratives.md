## Demonstrative Adjectives

Demonstratives point things out. English has two distances (this / that); Spanish has **three**:

| Distance | Masc. sing. | Fem. sing. | Masc. pl. | Fem. pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| near the speaker (this / these) | *este* | *esta* | *estos* | *estas* |
| near the listener, or a bit away (that / those) | *ese* | *esa* | *esos* | *esas* |
| far from both (that ... over there) | *aquel* | *aquella* | *aquellos* | *aquellas* |

They agree with the noun in gender and number, and normally go **before** it.

» *Este libro es mío.* — This book is mine.
» *¿Te gusta esa camisa?* — Do you like that shirt (near you)?
» *Aquellas montañas son altísimas.* — Those mountains (over there) are very high.

### Distance in time

The same idea works with time: *este año* (this year), *esa semana* (that week, recently mentioned), *en aquella época* (back in those days, long ago).

### After the noun

Placed after the noun, a demonstrative sounds emphatic or even scornful:

» *El chico ese no me cae bien.* — I don't like that guy.

> [!hack] Este has no -o
> The masculine singular forms end in *-e* and *-el*: *este, ese, aquel*. ~~esto libro~~ → *este libro*. The *-o* forms are neutral pronouns (see the next lesson).

> [!fact] Aquí, ahí, allí
> The three adverbs of place line up with the three demonstratives: *aquí* (here) — *este*, *ahí* (there, near you) — *ese*, *allí / allá* (over there) — *aquel*.

## Demonstrative Pronouns

A **demonstrative pronoun** replaces the noun: "this one", "those". It uses the same forms as the adjective.

» *No quiero este, quiero ese.* — I don't want this one, I want that one.
» *¿Cuáles prefieres, estas o aquellas?* — Which do you prefer, these or those over there?
» *Mi coche es aquel.* — My car is that one over there.

The pronoun agrees with the noun it stands for: if you mean *camisa*, use *esta, esa, aquella*.

### Accents

Older texts write the pronouns with an accent (*éste, ésa, aquél*) to set them apart from the adjectives. Current spelling rules say the accent is no longer needed, though you'll still see it.

### Neutral forms: *esto, eso, aquello*

These have no plural and don't refer to a specific noun. Use them for ideas, situations, or objects you can't name:

» *¿Qué es esto?* — What is this?
» *Eso es verdad.* — That's true.
» *Aquello fue un desastre.* — That (whole thing) was a disaster.
» *Por eso no vine.* — That's why I didn't come.

> [!trap] Esto never takes a noun
> ~~esto coche~~ → *este coche*. *Esto, eso* and *aquello* always stand alone.

### "The former" and "the latter"

*Este* can mean "the latter" (the nearer in the text) and *aquel* "the former": *Hablé con Ana y Luis; este estaba contento, aquella no.*

## Comprehensive Review 1

| | Adjective | Pronoun | Neutral |
|---|---|---|---|
| near me | *este / esta / estos / estas* + noun | *este, esta...* | *esto* |
| near you | *ese / esa / esos / esas* + noun | *ese, esa...* | *eso* |
| far | *aquel / aquella / aquellos / aquellas* + noun | *aquel, aquella...* | *aquello* |

Choose the right form:

» *___ (this) mesa* → *esta mesa*
» *___ (those, near you) zapatos* → *esos zapatos*
» *___ (that, over there) edificio* → *aquel edificio*
» *___ (these) flores* → *estas flores*
» *¿Qué es ___ (this, unknown object)?* → *esto*

## Comprehensive Review 2

Replace the noun phrase with a pronoun:

» *Prefiero este vestido.* → *Prefiero este.*
» *¿Ves aquellas casas?* → *¿Ves aquellas?*
» *Compramos esos tomates.* → *Compramos esos.*

Translate:

» I don't understand that. → *No entiendo eso.*
» This week I'm busy. → *Esta semana estoy ocupado.*
» Those (over there) are my parents. → *Aquellos son mis padres.*
» In those days we had no internet. → *En aquella época no teníamos internet.*

## Extra Practice 1

Focus: agreement.

» *___ (this) problema* → *este problema* (masculine)
» *___ (that) foto* → *esa foto* (feminine)
» *___ (those, far) días* → *aquellos días*
» *___ (these) manos* → *estas manos*
» *___ (that) agua* → *esa agua* (*agua* takes *el*, but demonstratives stay feminine)

## Extra Practice 2

Focus: distance. Choose *este*, *ese* or *aquel*.

» You're holding a pen: *___ bolígrafo es azul.* → *Este*
» Your friend holds a cup: *¿Es tuya ___ taza?* → *esa*
» A castle on a distant hill: *___ castillo es medieval.* → *Aquel*
» Talking about your childhood: *En ___ tiempos...* → *aquellos*

## Extra Practice 3

Focus: neutral pronouns and mixed review.

» *¿Qué es ___ que tienes en la mano?* → *eso*
» *Todo ___ que pasó ayer fue raro.* → *aquello* (or *eso*)
» *___ es muy importante: escucha.* → *Esto*
» *No quiero estos guantes; quiero ___ (those, near you).* → *esos*

> [!trap] Eso es + plural?
> *Eso* stays singular even if it refers to several things: *Eso es todo.* — That's all.
