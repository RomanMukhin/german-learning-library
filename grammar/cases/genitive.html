Genitive

The case of ownership and "of": forms with -s/-es, des and der, the genitive prepositions and why speech prefers von.

The genitive answers wessen? (whose?) and expresses "of": das Auto meines Vaters, das Ende des Films. It is the case you will meet most in written German: news, books, official letters. In everyday speech many Germans replace it with von + dative, so you need to recognise it well and use it in a few fixed patterns.

A2 core

Names: the easy genitive

With names the genitive is simply -s, without an apostrophe (unlike English):

  • Annas Bruder — Anna's brother
  • Peters Wohnung — Peter's flat
  • Deutschlands Hauptstadt — Germany's capital

Names ending in -s, -ß, -x, -z take an apostrophe: Klaus' Auto, Max' Hund. In speech people prefer das Auto von Klaus.

The forms

masculine feminine neuter plural
definite des Mannes der Frau des Kindes der Kinder
indefinite eines Mannes einer Frau eines Kindes –
possessive meines Vaters meiner Mutter meines Autos meiner Eltern

Only masculine and neuter nouns get an ending, -s or -es. Feminine and plural nouns stay unchanged.

  • das Auto meines Vaters — my father's car (машина мого батька)
  • die Farbe des Hauses — the colour of the house
  • das Ende der Geschichte — the end of the story
  • die Zimmer der Kinder — the children's rooms
  • der Name des Hotels — the name of the hotel

-es or -s?

Use -es after one-syllable nouns and after -s, -ß, -x, -z, -sch: des Mannes, des Tages, des Hauses, des Fußes, des Tisches. Use -s for longer nouns: des Vaters, des Lehrers, des Computers, des Problems. With short nouns both often work (des Tags / des Tages), so you cannot go badly wrong.

B1

Genitive prepositions: wegen, trotz, während, statt

Preposition Meaning Example
wegen because of Wegen des Regens bleiben wir zu Hause.
trotz despite Trotz der Kälte gehen wir spazieren.
während during Während des Urlaubs habe ich viel gelesen.
statt / anstatt instead of Statt eines Autos kaufe ich ein Fahrrad.

Also common in writing: innerhalb (within), außerhalb (outside), aufgrund (due to). Innerhalb einer Woche = within a week.

Genitive vs von in speech

Written / formal Spoken
das Haus meines Bruders das Haus von meinem Bruder
wegen des Wetters wegen dem Wetter
trotz des Regens trotz dem Regen

von + dative is always correct when there is no article (der Preis von Häusern) and with names (ein Freund von Anna). With prepositions, the dative (wegen dem) is accepted in everyday speech but marked as colloquial; in a letter, exam or essay use the genitive.

The pronoun escape

With pronouns, the genitive is rare and sounds stiff (wegen meiner). Use the variant with -etwegen: meinetwegen (because of me / fine by me), deinetwegen, seinetwegen. Meinetwegen! is also an everyday shrug: "I don't mind".

Word order

The genitive normally comes after the noun it describes: die Hauptstadt der Ukraine. Only names go in front: Romans Auto. Des Vaters Auto is poetic or old-fashioned.

Classic mistakes

  • Anna~~'~~s Buch — the standard spelling has no apostrophe: Annas Buch. Shop signs like Anna's Café are tolerated, but don't copy them.
  • das Auto ~~des Vater~~ — masculine needs the -s: des Vaters.
  • die Farbe ~~der Hauses~~ — neuter takes des, not der: des Hauses.
  • Weak nouns take -n, not -s: des Studenten, des Kollegen, des Herrn. See N-declension.
  • wegen ~~der Wetter~~ — das Wetter is neuter: wegen des Wetters (or in speech wegen dem Wetter).

Ukrainian vs German

The Ukrainian родовий (книга брата, кінець фільму) has the same job, and in the same position: after the noun. Книга мого брата = das Buch meines Bruders. But Ukrainian also uses the родовий for quantities and negation (багато людей, немає часу), and German does not: viele Leute, keine Zeit.

"Der Dativ ist dem Genitiv sein Tod"

In 2004 the journalist Bastian Sick published a book with this title, a collection of his Spiegel Online column "Zwiebelfisch" about German usage. It became one of the bestselling German non-fiction books of the decade and grew into a series. The title is a joke: dem Genitiv sein Tod ("to the genitive his death") is a colloquial dative construction replacing der Tod des Genitivs, so the sentence demonstrates exactly what it complains about.

Later: genitive in formal writing

In formal texts you will also see genitive objects of a few verbs (Wir gedenken der Opfer), genitive adverbs (eines Tages, meines Erachtens) and long genitive chains: die Ergebnisse der Untersuchung des Ministeriums. Recognise them; don't build them yet. See Formal German.
